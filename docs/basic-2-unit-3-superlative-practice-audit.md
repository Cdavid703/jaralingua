# Basic English Course 2 — Unit 3 Superlative Practice Audit

## Activities added

1. **The Superlative Wall** — guided visual speaking with three place groups.
2. **Superlative Grammar Challenge** — 15 A/B/C questions with checked feedback.

## Pedagogical content audit

Both activities use only material explicitly taught on `ingles/basico-2/unit-3-around-the-world.html`:

- countries, cities, landmarks, mountains, forests, and travel places;
- adjectives: big, cold, hot, old, modern, famous, beautiful, important, interesting, crowded;
- `the + adjective-est` and spelling patterns;
- `the most + adjective`;
- irregular form `the best`;
- `one of the most + adjective + plural noun`;
- `in` for a place group and `of` for a defined group.

## Content deliberately excluded

The Intermediate 1 originals include measurements, world-record facts, broader historical vocabulary, and extended justification production. These Basic 2 adaptations do **not** introduce measurements, numerical facts, record language, or new grammar.
