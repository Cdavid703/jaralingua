# Basic English 2 — Pronunciation Library

Route: ingles/basico-2/pronunciation-library.html. Home card and quick-menu entry immediately after Listening Library.

## Required catalog rule

Every new complete Basic English 2 pronunciation activity must appear BOTH in its unit's Practice Lab folder and in this library. Link to the SAME original activity page: do not clone the activity, rename its identifier, reset recordings, alter submissions, or change its gradebook behavior. Phonetic Rules remains the separate explanation resource. Do not mix conversation coaches or isolated vocabulary audio buttons into this library.

Initial catalog: Unit 1 Weather and Going Out; Unit 2 Shopping and Concert; Unit 3 Around the World; Unit 4 Regular -ed Pronunciation Studio; Unit 5 Looking Back Pronunciation Studio. Cards reuse the original activity images so students recognize the same activity. The library itself has a new exclusive professional hero, also used by its home card.

## Design contract

Full available screen width with safe gutters. Compact horizontal desktop hero in normal scrolling flow, never sticky/fixed on tablets/phones. Small QR INSIDE the hero title/description card, enlarged centrally by the shared QR component. Top-navigation sign-in only; no floating sign-in. No expanded-by-default details, empty units, 0% labels or long card descriptions. Each card has an image, unit, exact activity title, up to two lines of description, and Open activity. Grid: 4 columns from 1200px, 3 at 901–1199px, 2 at 480–900px, 1 below 480px for title readability.

When adding an activity, update the visible count and run tools/test_basic2_pronunciation_library.cjs. The test compares the complete pronunciation-page inventory, Practice Lab links and library links to catch omissions.
