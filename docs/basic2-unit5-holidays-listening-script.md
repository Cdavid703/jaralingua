## My Holidays
File: `my-holidays.mp3`

Daniel: Hi, Valeria! It's good to see you again. Where did you go on vacation?

Valeria: Hi! I went to Santa Marta for five days.

Daniel: Nice! Who did you go with?

Valeria: My sister. Our parents stayed in Medellin because they were working.

Daniel: Where did you stay?

Valeria: In a small hotel near the beach. It wasn't expensive, and the people were friendly.

Daniel: What was the weather like?

Valeria: It was sunny most days, but it rained on Tuesday. We stayed inside that morning.

Daniel: What did you do there when it was sunny?

Valeria: We swam and walked along the beach. In the evenings, we hung out at a little cafe. We didn't go shopping.

Daniel: That sounds relaxing. Who did you visit?

Valeria: My uncle. He lives there, and he cooked fish for us one evening.

Daniel: Was there a problem during your vacation?

Valeria: Yes, a small one on Wednesday. My sister wasn't well.

Daniel: Oh no! What happened?

Valeria: She felt sick after a long boat trip. We went back to the hotel and called it a night. She was fine the next morning.

Daniel: I'm glad she felt better. Why did you stay home for the last few days of your holiday?

Valeria: After we returned to Medellin, I needed to study for an English exam. I wasn't sick!

Daniel: So, how was your vacation overall?

Valeria: It was wonderful, even with that problem. The best part was spending time with my sister. And you?

Daniel: I stayed home, watched movies, and fixed my bike. It was quiet, but I enjoyed it.

Valeria: A quiet holiday sounds good too!
