# Food Quantities — brief quantifier reminder

Requested addition: a few (interpreting the dictated “fee”), some, many, much and a little. English-only quick reference before the existing questions, in a closed-by-default panel to avoid extra scrolling.

Five compact cards distinguish plural countable nouns from uncountable amounts, with food examples. Some includes positive sentences, offers and requests. Many/much are illustrated with questions and negatives, without falsely forbidding positive uses. A final note contrasts a few/a little with few/little and explains that a lot of is often more natural in everyday positive sentences.
