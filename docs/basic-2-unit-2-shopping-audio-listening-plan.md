## Activity identity

- **Activity title:** At the Concert Entrance
- **Course:** Basic English Course 2
- **Unit:** Unit 2 - Shopping Experiences
- **Practice Lab number:** Activity 04
- **Activity type:** Audio Listening
- **Estimated classroom time:** 15-20 minutes
- **Student language level:** Basic / A1+

## Pedagogical objective

Students listen to the second part of the Unit 2 story. The previous reading ends when Lucas receives a message at the concert entrance. This listening continues that moment and requires students to identify clothes, tickets, locations, and the final action.

By the end of the activity, students should be able to:

- understand the main situation in a short audio conversation;
- identify clothing descriptions in context;
- understand location clues around a concert entrance;
- recognize `this`, `that`, `these`, and `those` naturally in speech;
- answer ten multiple-choice comprehension questions without a visible answer pattern.

## Questions and answer distribution

Correct answer sequence:

`B, C, A, B, C, A, C, B, A, C`

This avoids an obvious repeated answer pattern.

1. What does Lucas receive at the concert entrance?  
   **B.** A message from Noah.

2. Where is Noah at first?  
   **C.** At the wrong entrance near a coffee stand.

3. What is behind Noah?  
   **A.** Two large posters.

4. What can Sofia see from their entrance?  
   **B.** The posters across the street.

5. What is Noah wearing?  
   **C.** A gray sweater and black pants.

6. How does Lucas describe Sofia to Noah?  
   **A.** She is wearing a blue jacket and jeans.

7. Where are Sofia and Lucas waiting?  
   **C.** Near the ticket line.

8. What does Sofia ask about the tickets?  
   **B.** If they are for this entrance.

9. Why should Noah not buy coffee now?  
   **A.** The concert starts in ten minutes.

10. Who does Sofia see next to the coffee stand?  
   **C.** Noah.
