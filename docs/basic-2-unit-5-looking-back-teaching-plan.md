# Basic English Course 2 - Unit 5: Looking Back

## Scope

This is the Course Overview teaching page for Unit 5. Practice Lab activities, assessments, and teacher deliverables are intentionally out of scope until planned separately.

## Pacing-guide alignment

- My first experiences and vacation memories.
- Expressions with `go` and `get`.
- Funny or embarrassing stories.
- Feelings and a short recount of what happened and why.

## Primary grammar focus

The central new teaching focus is the past of `be`:

- `was / were` in affirmative statements.
- `wasn't / weren't` in negative statements.
- Yes/no and `wh-` questions with `was / were`.
- Short answers.
- Present comparison: `am / is / are` for now versus `was / were` for before now.
- Past states (`I was nervous`) contrasted with past actions (`I went home`) and reactions (`I felt proud`).

Simple past action forms and sequencing words are reused from Unit 4; no present perfect, past continuous, or `used to` is introduced.

## Teaching structure

1. First experiences and memories.
2. Past-time signals.
3. Present versus past `be`.
4. Affirmative `was / were`.
5. Negative `wasn't / weren't`.
6. Questions and short answers.
7. States, actions, and reactions.
8. Feelings and `because`.
9. Vacation-memory irregular verbs.
10. Expressions with `go` and `get`.
11. Safe funny or embarrassing anecdotes.
12. Short-memory organization.
13. Unit phrasal verbs and idioms.
14. Can-do checklist.

Every teaching topic is a closed-by-default `<details>` section and begins with “What we will learn”.
