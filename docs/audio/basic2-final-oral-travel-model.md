# Basic 2 final oral exam — professional model

## A trip to Cartagena
File: `model-cartagena.mp3`

Server: Hello! Welcome. How can I help you?
Customer: Hello. I want to take a trip with my family. We are thinking about London or Cartagena. I like the sea and old buildings. Can you tell me about Cartagena?
Server: Cartagena is a city in Colombia, near the sea. In this picture, there are colorful buildings and streets. You can visit the old town, walk with your family and take photos.
Customer: That sounds good. What can I do near the sea?
Server: You can walk by the sea. You can also visit a beach. Which activity do you prefer?
Customer: I like walking and taking photos. Can I visit the old town and the beach on my trip?
Server: Yes, you can. You can visit the old town on one day and go to the beach on another day. You can’t visit every place in one afternoon.
Customer: Can you repeat the last part, please?
Server: Of course. You can’t visit every place in one afternoon. Take your time and choose a few activities.
Customer: Thank you. Is there any food to try?
Server: Yes. You can try local food, such as fish and rice. What food do you like?
Customer: I like fish, but my sister doesn’t. Can she choose something different?
Server: Yes, she can ask about other dishes at a restaurant. You can ask, “What is in this dish?”
Customer: What can we see in the picture? I can see some colorful buildings. Is there a place to walk with my family?
Server: Yes. Look at the street in the picture. You can walk there and look at the buildings. You can also take photos and stop for a drink when you need a break.
Customer: My father likes taking photos, and my mother likes trying different food. I think there are things for all of us to do. What can we do on a rainy day?
Server: You can choose an indoor activity. For example, you can visit a museum or have lunch at a restaurant. You can talk about your plans and go for a walk later.
Customer: That sounds nice. I still like London, but for this trip I prefer Cartagena because we want to be near the sea. I want to show the picture to my family.
Server: Of course. Tell them about the old town, the beach and the food. What is your final decision?
Customer: Great. I want to visit the old town first because I like colorful buildings. Then I want to walk by the sea.
Server: That sounds like a good plan. Enjoy your trip!
Customer: Thank you for your help. Goodbye!
