# Lunch at Maple Café

## Restaurant conversation
File: `maple-cafe.mp3`

Server: Welcome to Maple Café. Are you ready to order?
Customer: Yes, please. I'd like a bowl of vegetable soup. Is there any cream in it?
Server: No cream. It has carrots, potatoes, and a little salt.
Customer: Great. And two slices of bread, please.
Server: I'm sorry, we ran out of bread. Would you like some rice instead?
Customer: Just a little rice, please. Is the soup spicy?
Server: No, it's mild. What would you like to drink?
Customer: A glass of water, please. No ice.
Server: Anything for dessert? Today's special is chocolate cake.
Customer: I have a sweet tooth! A small piece to take home, please, not to eat here.
Server: Of course. That's twelve dollars altogether.
Customer: Here you are. Thank you!
