# Making Friends teaching media

## Professional audio

The canonical 43-model inventory is `ingles/preintermedio-1/audio/unit1/lesson/audio-manifest.json`. Exact scripts are in the same folder. Catalog voice assignments are in `tools/elevenlabs_voice_cast.preintermediate1-unit1.json`. Each dialogue turn is generated separately with its assigned voice; role labels are not spoken. The full conversation button plays those same turns in order.

The teaching page and expression library use the same audio data. Playback has one active player, 0.75× and 1.0× speeds, stop, replay and recoverable error text. It stops when the page is hidden. These teaching models do not record students or create grades.

## Original image

Mode: built-in ImageGen. Project asset: `assets/img/english-preintermediate-1/unit-1/making-friends-hero.webp`. Original dimensions: 1536 × 1024. The same unit image identifies its home and overview route; future separate activities require their own images.

Prompt used

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: horizontal hero photograph for the Making Friends unit of a pre-intermediate adult English course on Jaralingua. Primary request: candid professional editorial photograph of three adult Colombian English learners meeting for the first time in a bright contemporary classroom in Medellin, two women and one man approximately 25 to 45, exchanging names and interests warmly while seated around a small table with notebooks. Scene: real adult language classroom with soft daylight, plants and neutral furnishings, welcoming and respectful, natural expressions and gestures, realistic hands. Composition: wide landscape photograph, balanced eye-level framing, faces clearly visible, no large empty areas. Color: warm daylight with subtle teal and navy clothing accents consistent with a professional educational website. Constraints: no legible writing, no logos, no watermark, no graphic overlays, no children. Create one landscape image.

The web interface uses the original Basic 2 palette and shared components. Clothing colours in the photograph do not define interface colours.
