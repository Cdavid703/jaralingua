## Pedagogical objective

Students read the first part of a short two-part story. The reading uses Unit 2 shopping language in context and prepares the narrative for a later listening activity that will continue the story.

By the end of the activity, students should be able to:

- understand the main shopping situation;
- identify clothes and accessories in context;
- recognize `this`, `that`, `these`, and `those` naturally;
- understand size, fit, price, and buying decisions;
- answer ten multiple-choice comprehension questions without relying on a visible answer pattern.

## Narrative connection

- **Reading Part 1:** Sofia and Lucas shop for a jacket before a concert.
- **Future Listening Part 2:** The story continues when Lucas receives a message at the concert entrance.

The final reading sentence creates the listening bridge:

> At the concert entrance, Lucas receives a message from their friend. The night is about to change.

## Reading text

**A Jacket for the Concert**

Sofia and Lucas are going to a small concert tonight. Before the show, they stop at a clothing store because Sofia needs a warmer jacket. Her sweater is too light for the cool evening.

Near the entrance, Sofia sees a blue jacket. "This jacket is nice," she says. Lucas points across the store. "That black jacket looks warmer, but it may cost more." Sofia tries the blue jacket in small, but it feels tight. She asks, "Do you have this in medium?" The assistant brings a medium size.

While Sofia checks the mirror, Lucas looks at sunglasses near the counter. "These sunglasses are cool," he says, "but they are not useful for tonight." Sofia checks the price: forty-five dollars. It is not cheap, but it fits well and matches her jeans. She buys the jacket.

At the concert entrance, Lucas receives a message from their friend. The night is about to change.

## Questions and answer distribution

Correct answer sequence:

`C, A, B, C, A, C, B, A, C, B`

This avoids a repeated A/B/C pattern.

1. Why do Sofia and Lucas stop at the clothing store?  
   **C.** Sofia needs a warmer jacket.

2. Why is Sofia's sweater not enough?  
   **A.** It is too light for the cool evening.

3. What does Sofia see near the entrance?  
   **B.** A blue jacket.

4. What does Lucas say about the black jacket?  
   **C.** It looks warmer, but it may cost more.

5. What is the problem with the first blue jacket Sofia tries?  
   **A.** It feels tight.

6. What size does Sofia ask for?  
   **C.** Medium.

7. Where are the sunglasses?  
   **B.** Near the counter.

8. Why does Lucas not need the sunglasses for the evening?  
   **A.** They are not useful for tonight.

9. Why does Sofia buy the jacket?  
   **C.** It fits well and matches her jeans.

10. What happens at the concert entrance?  
   **B.** Lucas receives a message from their friend.
