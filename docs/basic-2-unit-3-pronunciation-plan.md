# Basic English Course 2 — Unit 3 Pronunciation: Around the World

## Purpose

This Unit 3 Practice Lab activity develops clear pronunciation of precise place adjectives, comparative forms, superlatives, and short connected descriptions. It is a teacher-visible follow-up worth **0%**: it is recorded in the gradebook but never changes the accumulated grade.

## Required experience

- Six guided sections plus one cumulative final challenge that contains all six sections.
- Professional American-English ElevenLabs model for each section.
- Every visible token is a clickable word button. Each unique word plays its own dedicated ElevenLabs MP3 at `audio/unit3/pronunciation/words/`; there is no browser `speechSynthesis` fallback.
- The student may slow section models to 0.75 or play at 1.0, use Shadow mode, record repeatedly, receive word-level transcription feedback, and replay a missed word alone.
- English transcription endpoint: `/api/english-basic/pronunciation-assessment` only.
- Authenticated student can send the completed seven-stage report to `/api/basic2/unit3-pronunciation-around-world/submit`.
- Report records scores, transcript and missed words as a Unit 3 follow-up in the Basic 2 gradebook (weight 0).

## Section language

1. `ancient, historic, modern, traditional.`
2. `crowded, quiet, peaceful, remote.`
3. `scenic, spacious, touristy, impressive.`
4. `Lake Miran is quieter than Valeria. Valeria is more crowded at noon. The hotel is more spacious than the hostel.`
5. `The tower is the most impressive landmark. Lake Miran is one of the quietest places in the guide. The main square is the most crowded place at noon.`
6. `Lake Miran is peaceful and scenic. Valeria is historic and touristy. The lake village is more remote, but the city has an impressive museum.`
7. Final challenge: all previous sections in order.
