# Basic English Course 2 — Unit 4 Grammar Practice

## Activity

**Regular -ed: Spelling and Sound Lab** is the first Practice Lab activity for Unit 4, Busy Lives. It is formative only: no sign-in requirement, no teacher delivery, and weight 0%.

## Objective

Learners choose accurate regular past forms and identify whether `-ed` is pronounced `/t/`, `/d/`, or `/ɪd/`.

## Structure

1. Eight A/B/C spelling questions: add `-ed`, final `-e + d`, consonant doubling, and consonant + `y → ied`.
2. Six A/B/C sound questions with a dedicated ElevenLabs word model.
3. Four combined questions that require the written form and ending sound together.

Every distractor is a plausible spelling or sound error. Correct answer positions are mixed, and no Spanish translation appears in student questions.

## Feedback and audio

- **Check answers** gives rule-specific feedback for all 18 questions.
- **Try again** resets the full activity and supports unlimited attempts.
- Sound and combined questions reuse the 21 Unit 4 ElevenLabs models. Each listening question plays one dedicated word clip, never browser speech synthesis.
- Playback offers 0.75× and 1.0× speed.
