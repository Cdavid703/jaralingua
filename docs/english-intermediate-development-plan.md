### Ciclo pedagogico obligatorio por actividad

Toda actividad nueva debe seguir este ciclo para evitar paginas sueltas o tareas demasiado abiertas:

| Etapa | Funcion | Como se ve en una pagina |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input | Dar un modelo antes de pedir produccion. | Audio, lectura corta, dialogo, ficha de datos, ejemplo de blog, tabla o mini video. |
| Noticing | Hacer visible el idioma que se quiere practicar. | Palabras clave, gramatica en contexto, expresiones utiles, pronunciacion focalizada, preguntas de observacion. |
| Practice | Controlar el idioma antes de abrir la tarea. | Gap fills, matching, categorizar, sentence builder, tarjetas de datos, preguntas guiadas. |
| Communicative task | Llevar el idioma a interaccion real de clase. | Role play, debate, survey, gallery walk, pitch, entrevista, mediacion o decision grupal. |
| Product/feedback | Cerrar con evidencia evaluable. | Parrafo, blog, ficha, audio oral, presentacion, decision justificada, peer feedback o rubrica corta. |

## What To Keep

- Unit 1 has a strong narrative ecosystem: stereotypes, superhero/supervillain, listening, reading, case briefing, pronunciation.
- Unit 2 has useful productive tasks: dreams, timelines, catching-up listening, pronunciation.
- Unit 3 already has a good visual base: superlative wall, grammar challenge, wonder presentation, pronunciation.
- The Practice Lab folder model from Basic English is the right structure to apply across Intermedio.

## Activities To Rework

- The linked Basic `Guess Who Game: Professions Edition` in Unit 1 is below intermediate level as-is. Keep the format only if upgraded with hedging language, evidence, stereotype reflection, and follow-up explanations.
- The original "picture-based discussion" for wishes/hopes is too open. It should become a guided inference task with language frames: `He might be hoping to...`, `She has always wanted to...`, `The obstacle seems to be...`.
- Unit 3 "country wonders rating chart" can become guesswork unless students receive fact cards. Add data cards with height, length, age, location, and one surprising fact.
- Unit 3 "commercial video" is useful but too broad. Turn it into a storyboard-and-pitch workshop with a 60-90 second script, required superlatives, and peer voting criteria.
- Unit 4 family-law debate needs careful framing. Avoid personal disclosure or violent wording in student-facing prompts; use fictional family scenarios, respectful disagreement, and solution language.
- Unit 6 "psychologist role play" should be reframed as a life-coach or peer-advice clinic. Students can discuss routines and advice without pretending to diagnose mental-health issues.
- Unit 5 food tasks can become shallow menu lists. Require quantities, cultural comparison, ingredients, preparation, price, health angle, and an opinion/review.

## Minimum Structure Per Unit

Each unit from Unit 3 onward should include:

- Explanation page.
- Grammar/sentence builder.
- Listening workshop.
- Reading workshop.
- Pronunciation workshop.
- Speaking or role-play task.
- Writing/product task.
- Assessment or rubric support when the unit connects to a graded task.
