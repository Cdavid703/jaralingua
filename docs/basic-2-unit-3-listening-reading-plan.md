# Basic English Course 2 — Unit 3 Connected Listening and Reading

## Sequence

1. **Audio Listening: Choosing the Right Place** is Part 1. Nora and Eli compare Valeria, a historic capital, and Lake Miran, a peaceful village. The recording ends before they choose.
2. **Reading: The Final Choice** is Part 2. It adds guide details that are not stated in the audio and reveals their decision.

## Listening

- 14-turn dialogue, professional ElevenLabs US voices: Nora (Matilda) and Eli (Sean).
- Audio speed controls: 0.75x, 1.0x, 1.25x.
- Transcript download only for the administrator account.
- Ten A/B/C questions. Correct positions: B, A, C, A, B, C, B, A, C, A.
- Focus vocabulary: ancient, historic, modern, traditional, crowded, quiet, peaceful, remote, scenic, spacious, touristy, impressive.

## Reading

- 136 words — within the 150-word maximum.
- It does not repeat the audio; it adds the crowded-hour warning, hotel-room detail, photo recommendation, booking, and schedule.
- Ten A/B/C questions. Correct positions: B, C, A, C, B, A, B, C, A, C.
