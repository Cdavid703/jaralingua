# Basic 2 Unit 4 — Past Verbs: Step-by-Step Pronunciation

Route: ingles/basico-2/pronunciation-unit-4-past-verbs-step-by-step.html. A separate activity, not a replacement for Regular -ed Pronunciation Studio. Listed in Unit 4 Practice Lab (08) and Pronunciation Library.

## Pedagogy

Three blocks (/t/, /d/, /ɪd/), each ten isolated regular-past verbs followed by three short sentences: 39 scored attempts. Rules depend on the final SOUND of the base form. /t/ and /d/ endings do not add a syllable; /ɪd/ follows base /t/ and /d/ and adds one syllable. Learners may review any challenge, retry without limit, and use Next after evaluation even at zero. No concatenated final reading and no pace penalty.

Scores are word-recognition estimates, never claims of verified phoneme accuracy. Word-level alignment provides matched/missed highlighting. The best attempt per challenge counts; summaries show all three groups and the complete average. Technical analysis failures do not erase scores or become failed attempts. Audible speech with no recognized words may receive zero; silence requires checking the microphone. Model and individual words use the same 0.75×/1.0× speed selector.
