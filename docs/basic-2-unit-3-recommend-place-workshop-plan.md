# Basic English Course 2 — Unit 3

## Practice Lab Activity 09: Recommend a Place

### Purpose

An ungraded, teacher-led oral workshop for groups of three. Students apply the Unit 3 language to recommend one city, country, landmark, or natural place through a short visual presentation.

### Student outcome

Each group gives a 3–4 minute presentation that includes:

- One place and its country or location.
- At least two Unit 3 adjectives to describe it.
- One accurate comparative.
- One accurate superlative.
- A clear recommendation with a reason.

### Group roles

1. **Place guide:** introduces the place and two features.
2. **Comparison guide:** compares it with another place and names a suitable visitor.
3. **Recommendation guide:** uses a superlative, gives the reason, and closes.

Each learner speaks for approximately 45–60 seconds.

### Visual presentation

Groups prepare four image-led slides: place and country, characteristics, comparison, and final recommendation. Images support oral communication; learners should not read a dense written script.

### Assessment and delivery

The workshop does not create a submission, grade, percentage, or teacher queue. The teacher gives oral feedback on clarity, pronunciation, comparatives, superlatives, and teamwork.

### Page standards applied

- Professional, independent hero image.

- Mobile-responsive visual slide, role, model, and preparation grids.
