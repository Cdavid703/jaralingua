## Objective

Students practice clothing vocabulary, prices, numbers, and natural shopping questions:

- How much is the jacket?
- How much are the sunglasses?
- It's forty-eight dollars.
- They're twenty-eight dollars.

## Pedagogical flow

1. The teacher shows a clothing/accessory card with a visible price.
2. The teacher asks the correct price question.
3. The student answers orally.
4. The teacher reveals the model answer only after the student attempts the response.

The activity is oral and teacher-led. It does not collect written answers and it does not score students.
