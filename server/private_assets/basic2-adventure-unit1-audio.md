# Unit 1 adventure listening

## forecast
File: `forecast.mp3`

Narrator: Good morning. Here is the weather around our town. Near the river, it is sunny but cold. People are walking with warm jackets. In the city center, it is cloudy and windy. A few cyclists are waiting near the library. At the park, it is raining heavily. The outdoor tennis courts are closed, but the sports center is open. Some students usually play tennis outdoors on Sundays. Today, they are exercising inside. If you are near the river, take a jacket. If you are at the park, stay inside. The café next to the sports center is open too.

## change
File: `change.mp3`

Customer: Hi, Ben. Are you waiting at the park?
Server: Yes, I am. I usually run here on Sundays, but I am not running today. It is pouring.
Customer: I am looking out of my window. It is windy here too. Can we call off our picnic?
Server: Yes. I am packing up the food now. What about the library?
Customer: The library is closed today. Let’s meet up at the café next to the sports center.
Server: Great. Is your sister coming?
Customer: No. She feels under the weather, so she is staying in.
Server: Okay. I am walking to the café now. I am wearing a red jacket.
Customer: Perfect. I am taking my umbrella and leaving home.

## routine
File: `routine.mp3`

Narrator: My name is Nina. I usually work out at the sports center on Saturday mornings. My brother usually plays soccer outside, and my parents walk by the river. Today is different. It is stormy, so we are all staying at home. I am practicing English with my friend online. My brother is making sandwiches in the kitchen. He is not watching television. My parents are reading in the living room. My friend wants to come over, but the wind is very strong. I say, can we take a rain check and meet tomorrow? She says yes. We can still talk on the phone today. Every cloud has a silver lining.

## message
File: `message.mp3`

Customer: Hi, Leo. What are you doing?
Server: I am waiting outside the cinema. I usually come here on Fridays.
Customer: Are you wearing a blue coat?
Server: No, I am wearing a green jacket. It is cold and windy. Where are you?
Customer: I am sitting in the café across from the cinema. It is warm inside.
Server: Is Maya with you?
Customer: Yes. She is drinking tea and checking the movie times. The movie starts at six thirty.
Server: Great. It is only five thirty now. I can come over to the café.
Customer: Good idea. We are not buying tickets yet. Maya is waiting for her brother.
Server: Okay. I am crossing at the traffic lights now. See you in a minute.
