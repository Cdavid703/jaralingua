01

Making Friends

Sessions 1–2 · 6 hours

Meet people, describe personality and explain shared interests.

Making a first connection

Open a conversation, share a little information and leave space for the other person.

Names and identities

Use the name someone prefers and confirm exact information when you need it.

Personal information and routines

Choose useful information about your everyday life and answer with one extra detail.

Asking and extending questions

Choose be or do/does according to the main verb, then ask a question connected to the answer.

Likes and favorite activities

Say what you enjoy, express a preference and give a clear reason.

Personality in context

Describe yourself with an adjective and an example, and listen to how another person describes themselves.

Similarities and differences

Report what you share and what is different without needing identical answers.

Natural social expressions

Distinguish phrasal verbs, idiomatic language and ordinary social phrases, and use them for a real purpose.
