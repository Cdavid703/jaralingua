Exercise 1 · Routines and actions around now

Complete each gap with the verb form only. Use the present simple for a repeated routine, or the present continuous for an action happening now or a temporary change. Use the words in brackets.

Worked example

Every morning, I walk to work. Today, I am taking the bus.

Write only the missing verb form, including the subject where it appears in brackets. For example, (she / read) in a question becomes “is she reading” for an action happening now. Full forms and contractions are accepted.

02

Exercise 2 · How often?

Rewrite each sentence with the expression in brackets. Keep all the original words. Put a frequency adverb in its usual middle position: before the main verb, or after am, is or are. Put twice a week at the end.

Worked example

I usually cook at home. I am often hungry after work.

Other positions can be correct in English. Here, practise the middle position for adverbs and the end position for twice a week. Do not change the meaning or add words. Full forms and contractions are accepted.
