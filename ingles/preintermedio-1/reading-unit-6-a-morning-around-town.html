Part 2 · Mateo’s message to Laura

A Morning Around Town

Hi Laura,

1Here's our Saturday plan. Our meeting point is the tourist office at nine thirty. We're joining the guided walk at ten. It costs five dollars per person and lasts ninety minutes.

2First, the group visits the museum to see the craft exhibition. Then, we walk through the park. The tour ends at the market at eleven thirty.

3We can look around the stalls before lunch at noon. The market is open until six, so there's time to explore. I recommend bringing a camera and comfortable shoes.

4We can get around on foot. I checked the opening times: the museum is closed on Monday, but it is open from nine to five on Saturday. Keep the leaflet for the return trip. It also has the town map.

See you on Saturday!
Mateo

Three things to notice

  • The meeting point and the tour’s order.
  • Prices, opening times and the lunch plan.
  • Recommendations for getting around town.
