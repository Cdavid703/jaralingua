Part 2 · Friday · Laura’s class post

Small Changes, A Better Week

1I usually buy lunch near the bakery, but this week I'm bringing a packed lunch from home. I prepare it in the evening, so I have more time during my break.

2At twelve thirty, I meet Mateo near the school. We walk for ten minutes, then sit in the park. Our whole break lasts thirty minutes. When I feel under pressure, I take a break instead of checking work messages.

3My English project still has a deadline. Before studying, I write three tasks in my notebook and do one at a time. I'm trying to cut down on checking my phone.

4At home, I listen to quiet music to wind down. Next week, I'm going to keep the lunch plan and the short walks. I'm not going to change every habit at once.

Three things to notice

  • The changes Laura chooses for this week.
  • Meeting times, duration and a response to pressure.
  • Her intentions for next week.
