Exercise 2 · One serving or the whole bag?

Use the fictional snack label. Read the correct column for one serving or the whole bag. Write a number and its unit for each answer. Use grams, kilocalories or servings as appropriate. Numbers can be written as words or numerals.

Worked example

The bag contains two servings. One serving is thirty grams, so the whole bag is sixty grams.

Include both a number and the unit. For example, 30 g and thirty grams mean the same amount. Decimal points matter: 0.2 g is different from 2 g. A decimal comma is also accepted for 0,2 g and 0,4 g.

Exercise 3 · How much? How many?

For items one to four, complete each question with much or many. For items five to eight, use the fictional label to complete the answer with a number and its unit. Write only the missing word or amount.

Worked example

How much fat is there? How many grams are there? Use much with fat and many with grams.

Much goes with substances such as sugar and salt; many goes with countable servings or grams. For the answer gaps, include the requested number and unit.
