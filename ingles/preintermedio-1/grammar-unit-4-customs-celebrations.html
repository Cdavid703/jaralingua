Exercise 1 · Customs and frequency

Complete each gap with the present simple form. Use all the words in brackets. Include the subject when it appears in brackets. Put frequency adverbs before the main verb or after be. Write only the missing words.

Worked example

Our family usually shares a meal. The celebration is often at home.

Use the usual middle position for frequency adverbs: before the main verb or after be. Other positions can be correct in English, but this exercise practises that position. Keep all the words in brackets; do not repeat the words outside the gap. Full forms and contractions are accepted.

Exercise 2 · What is there?

Complete each gap with the missing words. Choose between the alternatives in brackets, or use the word in brackets to build the required expression. Write only the missing words.

Worked example

There are some plates on the table. There is a lot of rice in the bowl.

Use the alternatives or the word in brackets to complete the gap. Some and a lot of can both describe countable or uncountable nouns; follow the clue rather than guessing an amount. Keep words already outside the gap. Full forms and contractions are accepted.
