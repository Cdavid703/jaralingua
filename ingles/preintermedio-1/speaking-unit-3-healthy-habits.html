Food and fitness · Page 2 · Task 3

Task 3: Discuss the following questions as a class.

1. Can you name some foods you like to eat that contain a lot of carbohydrates?

2. How can you check how many calories are in the foods you eat?

3. Can you give some examples of processed foods? Do you like or dislike them?

4. Do you know anyone who follows a plant-based diet?

5. What are some aerobic exercises you can do to improve your heart health?

Review the original video activity

Task 3: Discuss the following questions as a class.

a. What physical activity do you do daily or weekly?

b. From the video, what are some benefits of physical activity?

c. What simple ways does the video suggest to add physical activity into our lives?

d. Do you go to the gym? Describe the positives and negatives of going to the gym.

e. What types of foods does the video suggest avoiding?

f. What is a balanced diet? Do you think you have a balanced diet?

g. What benefits are associated with quitting smoking?

h. Do you think smoking should be banned for all ages to improve people’s health?

i. Can you summarise the main ideas presented in the video?

Task 3: Discuss the following questions as a class.

1. Are you planning to cut down on something to improve your health?

2. Do you know anyone who needs to cut down on the amount of time they spend watching series on streaming services like Netflix?

3. Do you prefer to work out at home or at the gym?

4. Do you think social media encourages people to work out more?

5. Do you think you would be able to give up social media for one year?

6. Describe a time you gave up doing something that was bad for your health.

7. Do you have a morning and nighttime routine that you try to stick to?

8. Do you find it challenging to stick to a healthy eating plan?
