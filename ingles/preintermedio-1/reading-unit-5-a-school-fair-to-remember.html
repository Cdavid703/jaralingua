Part 2 · Mateo’s childhood memory

A School Fair to Remember

1When I was nine, our primary school held a fair on a Saturday. I used to make paper things after school, but that day I wanted to show my work to visitors.

2I made a blue and yellow kite. Before the fair opened, the string broke. I felt nervous because I didn't know how to fix it.

3A classmate named Sara came to my table. She found another piece of string and helped me tie a new knot. Then we checked the kite together and put it on the display table.

4Later, we showed our work to the visitors. I was proud of the kite and grateful for Sara's help. I don't make kites now, but I still enjoy making things with other people. I look back on that childhood memory with a smile.

Three things to notice

  • A past habit and one particular Saturday.
  • The problem, the help and the result.
  • What changes and what stays the same.
