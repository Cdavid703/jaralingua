Grammar reference · Can and can’t

Read the theory about the grammar point and then complete the exercises.

Auxiliary Verb

Can

Used to express something we know how to do.

I can cook.

I can sing.

Can’t

Used to express something we DON’T know how to do.

I can’t cook.

I can’t sing.

Form: subject + can / can’t + base verb. Use the same can with I, you, he, she, it, we and they. Do not add -s, -ing or to to the verb after can.

Questions: Can + subject + base verb? Negative: can’t or cannot. Both negative forms are accepted in Task 2. Keep each original sentence’s meaning when you correct it.
