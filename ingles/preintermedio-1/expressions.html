Meaning before memorising

A phrasal verb combines a verb with a particle. An idiom or idiomatic expression has a conventional meaning that you learn as a complete phrase. The Spanish line explains the general idea; the English example shows how to use it.

01

Making Friends

SpeedTap a word or sentence to listen.

Phrasal verb

llevarse bien con

Have a friendly relationship with someone.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Keep with before the person.

Phrasal verb

abrirse o hablar con más confianza

Start talking more openly about your thoughts or feelings.

Use: Conversational. People can take time to open up.

Idiom

romper el hielo

Help people feel more comfortable at the start of an interaction.

Use: Common in social and classroom situations.

Idiomatic expression

tener algo en común

Share an interest, characteristic or experience.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Name the shared interest when possible.

02

Interests and Hobbies

Understand the meaning and use of five expressions for hobbies.

SpeedTap a word or sentence to listen.

Phrasal verb

empezar una afición

Begin an activity as a hobby.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Put the activity after take up.

Phrasal verb

probar para ver cómo funciona

Test an activity, method or piece of equipment.

Use: Everyday, neutral. With a pronoun: try it out.

Phrasal verb

participar

Take part in an activity with other people.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Use join us for the people, or join in for participation.

Idiom

empezar a dominar algo

Learn how to do something through practice.

Use: Informal, common. Follow of with an activity or a noun.

Idiom

no ser lo mío

Say that something is not your preference.

Use: Conversational. It expresses a personal preference.

03

Healthy Living

Understand five expressions for habits, feelings and rest.

SpeedTap a word or sentence to listen.

Phrasal verb

reducir la cantidad o frecuencia de algo

Do or use less of something.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Keep on before the noun or activity.

Phrasal verb

dejar de hacer algo

Stop an activity or habit.

Use: Everyday, neutral in this use. Follow it with a noun or an -ing activity; with an object pronoun: give it up.

Phrasal verb

relajarse después de un periodo de actividad

Gradually relax after being busy.

Use: Everyday, conversational. Here wind is pronounced like the verb in wind a clock.

Idiom

sentirse un poco indispuesto

Feel slightly unwell.

Use: Informal, common. It describes a feeling without naming a specific illness.

Idiom

recuperar energías o descansar

Rest and feel ready for activity again.

Use: Conversational, figurative. Change your to my, her or our to match the person.

04

Celebrations and Festivals

Understand five expressions for gatherings, preparations and celebrations.

SpeedTap a word or sentence to listen.

Phrasal verb

preparar o disponer algo para usarlo

Arrange or prepare something so it is ready for use.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Set up the tables / set the tables up; with a pronoun: set them up.

Phrasal verb

colocar o colgar decoraciones

Place or hang something in position; here, decorations.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Put up the decorations / put the decorations up; with a pronoun: put them up.

Phrasal verb

reunirse

Meet and spend time with other people.

Use: Everyday, conversational. Get together with someone. The noun a get-together names a gathering.

Idiom

en pleno desarrollo o apogeo

Happening with a lot of activity.

Use: Everyday, conversational. Often follows be and describes an event in progress.

Idiom

pasarlo muy bien

Enjoy yourself very much.

Use: Informal, enthusiastic. It is not about a physical ball in this use.

05

Childhood Memories

Understand five expressions for childhood, remembering and looking back.

SpeedTap a word or sentence to listen.

Phrasal verb

crecer o pasar la infancia

Develop from a child into an adult; with a place, describe where someone spent childhood.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Past form: grew up. Grow up in a place.

Phrasal verb

recordar o reflexionar sobre una etapa pasada

Think about something that happened in the past.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Keep on before the period or experience.

Phrasal verb

hacer que vuelvan recuerdos

Make someone remember a past feeling or experience; here, bring back memories.

Use: Everyday, neutral in this use. Bring back happy memories / bring happy memories back; with a pronoun: bring them back.

Idiomatic expression

los viejos tiempos recordados con nostalgia

An earlier period remembered fondly, often with nostalgia.

Use: Conversational. It expresses a positive view of the past, not proof that everything was better then.

Idiom

un recorrido por los recuerdos

An occasion for remembering experiences from the past.

Use: Conversational, figurative. It does not describe a literal road or journey.

06

Around Town

Understand five expressions for exploring a town, transport and nearby places.

SpeedTap a word or sentence to listen.

Phrasal verb

desplazarse por una zona

Travel from place to place within an area.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Get around town by bus or on foot.

Phrasal verb

mirar o explorar un lugar

Explore a place and see what is there.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Look around the market or look around on your own.

Phrasal verb

subir a un autobús, tren u otro transporte similar

Board a bus, train or similar vehicle.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Get on the bus; for a car, get in the car is the usual expression.

Phrasal verb

bajar de un autobús, tren u otro transporte similar

Leave a bus, train or similar vehicle.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Get off the bus; for a car, get out of the car is the usual expression.

Idiom

a muy poca distancia

A very short distance.

Use: Conversational, figurative. It gives an approximate sense of closeness, not a precise measurement.
