Meaning before memorising

A phrasal verb combines a verb with a particle. An idiom or idiomatic expression has a conventional meaning that you learn as a complete phrase. The Spanish line explains the general idea; the English example shows how to use it.

SpeedTap a word or sentence to listen.

Phrasal verb

llevarse bien con

Have a friendly relationship with someone.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Keep with before the person.

Phrasal verb

abrirse o hablar con más confianza

Start talking more openly about your thoughts or feelings.

Use: Conversational. People can take time to open up.

Idiom

romper el hielo

Help people feel more comfortable at the start of an interaction.

Use: Common in social and classroom situations.

Idiomatic expression

tener algo en común

Share an interest, characteristic or experience.

Use: Everyday, neutral. Name the shared interest when possible.
