First Connections

1It is the first day of a new English course. Laura arrives a few minutes early and sits near Mateo. “Hi, I’m Laura. What’s your name?” she asks. Mateo introduces himself and spells his last name because Laura wants to write it correctly on her class card.

2They talk about their routines. Laura works in a bakery and studies English in the evening. Mateo works in a small shop. He usually reads after work, but Laura prefers listening to music. On weekends, they both like walking in the park.

3Their teacher asks everyone to find two shared interests. Laura and Mateo also like cooking, so they write that on their cards. They do not have the same daily routine, but they have something in common.

4Before class begins, Laura asks, “What do you usually cook?” Mateo gives an example and asks, “What about you?” Their first conversation is short, but both students ask questions and listen carefully.
