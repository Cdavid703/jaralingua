Part 2 · Continue the story

Something in Common

1After the conversation, Laura and Mateo finish their class cards. Laura describes her personality as talkative and creative. She likes sharing ideas and making bread. Mateo says he is quiet and patient. He listens carefully before speaking, and he does not interrupt other people.

2They notice a difference: Laura enjoys lively conversations, but Mateo prefers small groups. They take turns asking questions, so both have time to speak. Laura says she is organized because she prepares her bag every evening.

3Their second shared interest appears when Mateo talks about walking in the park on weekends. Laura enjoys that, too. They choose a short weekend walk as their activity. Cooking is still another interest they share.

4On their cards, they write two shared interests and one difference. Laura introduces Mateo to another classmate. They get along with each other because both listen and show respect.

Three things to notice

  • Personality supported by behavior.
  • A second shared interest.
  • The final activity they choose.
