Part 2 · Continue the story

Our First Group Project

1On Saturday, Laura joins Mateo's craft group. Ana welcomes her and puts two bracelets on the table. She asks Laura and Mateo to make a matching pair. Laura likes the blue one and chooses it as their model.

2First, they choose beads and arrange them in a simple pattern. Ana gives them thread and shows them how to begin. Laura listens to her carefully. Mateo can tie a knot, so Laura asks him for help.

3They work slowly and take turns using the tools. When the thread is too long, Ana cuts it. Laura writes the steps in her notebook to remember them.

4After two hours, their bracelets are ready. The colors match, but each bracelet has a different detail. Laura thanks the group. Making jewelry is new for her, but she is getting the hang of it.

Three things to notice

  • The project Laura and Mateo make together.
  • The people and objects behind the pronouns.
  • The steps and their purpose.
