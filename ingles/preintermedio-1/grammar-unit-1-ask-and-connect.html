Questions and short answers with do and does · Questions 4–6

4. Complete the question: “Where ___ you live?”

Mateo wants to know where Laura lives.

5. Complete the question: “___ Mateo work in a small shop?”

Laura works in a bakery. Mateo works in a small shop. Another learner asks about Mateo’s job.

6. Choose Mateo’s correct short answer.

Laura says, “I enjoy cooking.” A classmate asks Mateo, “Does Laura enjoy cooking?”

Likes and preferences

Enjoy, negative preferences and third-person forms · Questions 7–9

7. Complete the sentence: “I enjoy ___ after work.”

Laura talks about relaxing on weekdays.

8. Which sentence expresses Mateo’s dislike correctly?

Mateo says, “Noisy places are not enjoyable for me.” Laura reports his preference.

9. Complete Laura’s report: “Mateo ___ reading after work.”

Mateo likes both reading and listening to music, but reading is his first choice after work.

Personality and shared interests

Describe, explain and connect ideas · Questions 10–12

10. Which sentence describes the personality Mateo reports?

Mateo describes himself: “I’m quiet in large groups. I don’t talk much in that situation.”

11. Which sentence connects her description with the example she gives?

Laura says, “I’m organized. I prepare my bag every evening.”

12. Choose the sentence they can say together.

Laura enjoys cooking. Mateo enjoys cooking too. They identify this shared interest.

Keep the conversation going

Follow-up questions and a final exchange · Questions 13–15

13. Which follow-up question best fits her purpose?

Mateo says, “I enjoy cooking. I often make soup.” Laura wants one more detail about the soup.

14. Which follow-up question continues that topic?

Laura says, “I prefer listening to music on weekdays.” Mateo wants to learn about her musical tastes.

15. First ask about Mateo’s personality; then ask about Laura’s favorite activities.

Mateo: “I’m quiet and patient.” Laura: “I enjoy cooking and walking.” Choose questions that match the purpose and the answer in each turn.
