Part 2 · Friday · An email from Ana

Saturday’s Celebration Plan

Hi Laura and Mateo,

1Here is our final plan for Saturday's neighborhood celebration. We're meeting at the community hall at four to set up the tables. Laura is bringing bread from the bakery. Mateo and the craft group are putting up the decorations. Everyone agrees on these jobs and times.

2The guests are arriving at five. First, we welcome them at the door. Then, we share the food. There is some juice on the drinks table, and there are handmade cards beside the entrance.

3After the meal, the craft group is showing its work. Music starts at six, and everyone can join in. We're not using fireworks; this is a small indoor celebration.

4Please bring your notebook to check the tasks. We're going to take some photos for our group album.

See you on Saturday!
Ana

Three things to notice

  • The jobs and times agreed with the team.
  • The guests’ arrival and the order of activities.
  • What there is at the hall and the plan for photos.
