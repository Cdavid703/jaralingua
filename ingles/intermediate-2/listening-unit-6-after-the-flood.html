After checking your answers

Report a speaker’s words

Open the language reminder

Use said (that) + a reported statement. When reporting from a past viewpoint, present verbs often move back one tense: is → was, are → were, is rising → was rising.

Listen again. Who spoke? What did that person say? Begin: The rescue worker said that… or The mayor said that…

Report only the information you heard. Do not add a new detail.

Speak from what you heard

Report the news in 45 seconds

Tell your partner two facts from the bulletin and report what one person said. Keep facts separate from your own opinion.
