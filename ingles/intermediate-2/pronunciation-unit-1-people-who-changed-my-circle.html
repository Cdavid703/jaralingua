Pronunciation focus

How to sound clear and natural

neighbor_whoConnect the words smoothly; keep a light breath at the start of who.
that_I recentlyUse the voiced th /ð/ in that, then link the final t into I.
Iris, who...The commas mark extra information: use two short, meaningful pauses.
get_back_in_touchSay the expression as one connected meaning group.
get_on_wellLink the final consonants forward and stress well.
keep_in_touchConnect keep_in and finish touch with a clear /ch/ sound.
