Quick guide · must / might / can’t

must
I am almost sure it is true.
Strong positive deduction.

might
It is possible. I am not sure.
Other explanations are possible.

can’t
I am almost sure it is not true.
Strong negative deduction.

Use subject + modal + base verb: He must be tired. Here, must is a deduction, not an obligation; can’t is a deduction, not a rule or lack of ability.
