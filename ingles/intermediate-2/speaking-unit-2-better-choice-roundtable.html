Practice Lab · Unit 2 Group Speaking

The Better Choice Roundtable

Discuss one realistic dilemma in a small group. Share a hope or dream, weigh the consequences, give advice and agree on one practical next step.

Groups of 418 minutesSpeakingNo grade or submission

Activity goal

Make a choice together, not a speech alone.

Choose a fictional or real-life-friendly dilemma. Everyone speaks, listens and responds to another person. You do not need to share private information, record anything or send work to the teacher.

Success targetEvery person contributes two connected turns and the group ends with one shared recommendation.

1 · Set up the table

Give every voice a useful job.

Take one role each. After one dilemma, rotate roles if there is time.

Moderator

Opens the discussion, invites every person to speak and asks the final question.

“What are our options?”

Dreamer

Explains a hope, dream or polite desire behind one option.

“I hope to… / I dream of…”

Realist

Asks about consequences, limits and what could go wrong.

“What might happen if…?”

Adviser

Offers practical advice and helps name the next step.

“If I were you, I would…”

For 3 students: the Moderator also asks one Realist question. For 5 students: add an Evidence Keeper who summarizes one reason from each speaker.

2 · Choose one dilemma

Choose a situation worth discussing.

Tap one card. The situation is a starting point; your group can change small details and keep the conversation fictional.

No dilemma selected yet. Choose one card for your group.

3 · Speak in four rounds

Keep the conversation connected.

01

Open the case

Moderator: “We need to decide whether…” Each person adds one fact or question.

02

Name the hope

Dreamer: “I hope to… / I dream of…” The group asks one follow-up question.

03

Weigh the dilemma

Realist and Adviser: discuss one consequence, then offer one useful piece of advice.

04

Choose a next step

Everyone: agree on a realistic action, not a perfect answer.

Language support

Use one phrase, then add your own reason.

Hope and dreamsI hope to… / I hope we can… / I dream of…
Goals and desiresMy goal is to… / I would like to…
Wishes and regretsI wish I could… / I wish I had…
Advice and responseYou could… / If I were you, I would… / That makes sense because…
Follow-up questionsWhat would happen if…? / Why is that important? / What is the first step?
Group decisionWe think the better choice is… because…

4 · Final mini roundtable

How your group should finish

At the end, stay in the same roles and make one 60–90 second mini roundtable. It is spoken live, not a written report and not an evaluative delivery.

  1. Moderator: “We discussed [the dilemma].”
  2. Dreamer: “We hope to [goal] because…”
  3. Realist: “One consequence is…”
  4. Adviser: “We recommend [advice].”
  5. All together: “Our next step is…”
