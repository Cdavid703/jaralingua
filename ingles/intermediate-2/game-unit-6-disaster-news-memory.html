Build a short news report

Use the three pictures. Describe what happened, the effect and the help. Your partner asks a follow-up question. These are fictional practice situations.

Start: According to the news, …

Connect: As a result, … · After that, …

Ask: Where did it happen? · How were people affected? · What did the emergency services do?

Use keywords, then speak in your own words. Feedback comes from your partner or teacher.

Reflect on your practice

Can you name the pictures without help? Did you use a past-tense verb? Did you ask and answer a follow-up question?
