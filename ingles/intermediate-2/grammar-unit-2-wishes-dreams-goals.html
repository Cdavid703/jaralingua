Pattern map

Use these short reminders when feedback sends you back to a structure.

hope

hope + clause, hope to + verb, or hope for + noun.

I hope that they reply. / We hope to finish. / They hope for a change.
dream

dream of/about + noun / -ing.

She dreams of opening a studio.
wish

wish + past, could, would, or had + participle.

I wish I had more time. / I wish I could travel.
goal

goal is to + verb or goal of + -ing.

My goal is to improve. / Her goal of managing a team.
would like

would like + noun or would like to + verb.

I would like some advice. / I would like to talk.
make a wish

A fixed expression for a special desire.

Before the candles, everyone made a wish.
