Choose the expression inside the story

Maya could not find her notebook. She was ________ it.

looking for · looking at

The answer is looking for: she was trying to find it. Looking at means directing your eyes towards something you can already see.

Teacher guide

Read aloud with the class. At each blank, invite a student to choose one of the two expressions and explain the clue in the story. If the choice does not fit, discuss the feedback and let the student try again. Continue reading, then turn the page together.

Use the page menu to work in short sections. The final review revisits incorrect or unfinished challenges without changing the first-attempt score. This fictional news story supports vocabulary and oral practice.
