How speaking practice works

Listen, record and compare with the professional model. Automatic feedback checks the words the system understands; it does not measure individual sounds or accent.

When you record, your audio is sent to the Jaralingua server for recognition and discarded after processing. It is not submitted to your teacher or used for grades. You can practise without a microphone, but unverified turns do not earn the pronunciation trophy.
