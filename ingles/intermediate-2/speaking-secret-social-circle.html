Choose a secret relationship card, describe the person with who and that, and challenge your classmates to guess the connection.

Activity goal

Describe clearly. Guess carefully.

One student secretly chooses a relationship card and thinks of a real or fictional person. The student gives clues using relative clauses. The teacher chooses classmates to ask yes/no questions until someone guesses the relationship.

Build your clues

Give three to five clues

Do not say the relationship word until someone guesses correctly. Use clues that help your classmates identify the relationship little by little.

Start neutral

This is a person who...

This is a person who lives near me.

Add useful detail

This person is someone that...

She is someone that helps people in the neighborhood.

Sound tactful

Make the description tactful with a bit, kind of, or a little bit.

She is kind of quiet, but very helpful.

Useful language

For the student describing

This is a person who... This person is someone who... This is a person that... This person is kind of... This person is a bit... We get on well because... We keep in touch by... I got back in touch with this person...

Ask and guess

For the student who is guessing

The teacher chooses one student to ask a question. Ask one yes/no question. Do not shout the answer.

Interaction example

How one round sounds

Teacher: Ana, choose one secret card. Do not show it.

Ana: Ready. This is a person who lives near me. She is someone that helps people on my street. She is a little bit serious, but she is very responsible. She keeps the area clean and talks to people respectfully.

Teacher: Carlos, ask one question.

Carlos: Is she your close friend?

Ana: No, she isn't. She is not my close friend. She is someone I often see in my neighborhood.

Teacher: Daniela, ask one question.

Daniela: Is she your neighbor?

Ana: Yes, she is. She is my neighbor. She is the person who helps keep the street clean.

Classroom flow

One 10-minute cycle

  1. 1 minute: every student chooses one secret card.
  2. 2 minutes: every student prepares three to five clues.
  3. 6 minutes: the teacher selects one student to describe the person and then calls on guessers one at a time. After a correct guess, the teacher selects a new student.
  4. 1 minute: each student who described confirms the relationship, and the teacher notes who still needs a speaking turn.

Participation rule: Repeat the cycle until every student has described a person at least once.

Final confirmation model

Yes, Ana is my neighbor. She is a person who lives near me. She is someone that helps people on my street.

I used who as the clearest choice for a person. I used that only in a defining clause without commas. I answered the yes/no questions completely. I confirmed the relationship in a complete final response.
