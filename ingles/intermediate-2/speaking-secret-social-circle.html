Secret Social Circle

Choose a secret relationship, describe the person with who and that, and challenge your classmates to guess the connection.

Activity goal

Describe clearly. Guess carefully.

One student secretly chooses a relationship card and thinks of a real or fictional person. The student gives clues using relative clauses. The teacher chooses classmates to ask yes/no questions until someone guesses the relationship.

Build your clues

Give 3 to 5 descriptions

Do not say the relationship word at first. Use clues that help your classmates identify the person little by little.

Start neutral

This is a person who...

This is a person who lives near me.

Add useful detail

He / She is someone that...

She is someone that helps people in the neighborhood.

Sound tactful

He / She is a bit / kind of / a little bit...

She is kind of quiet, but very helpful.

Useful language

For the student describing

This is a person who... He / She is someone who... This is a person that... He / She is kind of... He / She is a bit... We get on well because... We keep in touch by... I got back in touch with this person...

Ask and guess

For the classmate guessing

The teacher chooses one student to ask a question. Ask one yes/no question. Do not shout the answer.

Is this person your neighbor?
Is he / she your close friend?
Is this person an old friend?
Is he / she your classmate?
Is this person a coworker?
Is this someone you met online?
Is this a person you lost contact with?
Is this a person you get on well with?

Interaction example

How one round sounds

Teacher: Ana, choose one secret card. Do not show it.

Ana: Ready. This is a person who lives near me. She is someone that helps people in my street. She is a little bit serious, but she is very responsible. She keeps the area clean and talks to people respectfully.

Teacher: Carlos, ask one question.

Carlos: Is she your close friend?

Ana: No, she isn't. She is not my close friend. She is a person who I see in my neighborhood.

Teacher: Daniela, ask one question.

Daniela: Is she your neighbor?

Ana: Yes, she is. She is my neighbor. She is the person who helps keep the street clean.

Classroom flow

10-minute version

  1. 1 minute: each student chooses one secret card.
  2. 2 minutes: students prepare 3 to 5 clues.
  3. 6 minutes: the teacher chooses guessers one by one.
  4. 1 minute: each round ends with a complete confirmation sentence.

Final confirmation model

Yes, Ana is my neighbor. She is a person who lives near me and that helps people in my street.

I used who for a person. I used that in an identifying clause. I answered the yes/no questions completely. I confirmed the relationship in one final sentence.
