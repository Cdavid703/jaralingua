# Intermediate English Course 2 — Unit 3 Listening Scripts

## The Message Before the Workshop
File: `the-message-before-the-workshop.mp3`

Camila: Daniel, sorry to call just before the workshop, but the laptop will not connect to the projector. The presentation app keeps loading, and I just received a support message.

Daniel: No problem. Could you tell me what the message says?

Camila: It says there is an urgent security update. It asked me to sign in through a link. The page looked official.

Daniel: Did you enter your password?

Camila: I did. Then the page froze, so I closed it. Was that a mistake?

Daniel: We need to act carefully. First, disconnect the laptop from Wi-Fi. Do not turn off the router because the other workshop devices still need the network.

Camila: The laptop is disconnected. Should I shut it down now?

Daniel: Not yet. Use your phone and open the official account app yourself. Do not use the link in the message. Could you tell me whether you see a sign-in alert?

Camila: Yes. It says someone signed in from another city three minutes ago. I do not recognize the device.

Daniel: Then the support message was probably a phishing attempt. You must not approve that session, and you should not put off changing your password.

Camila: Can I change it in the official app?

Daniel: Yes. After that, sign out of all other sessions and turn on two-step verification. Then look up the recent activity. Do not open any unexpected attachments.

Camila: Wait. A file was shared from my cloud drive, but I did not share it. There is also a new session, and it is still active.

Daniel: Camila, do not close the alert. Someone is still using your account.

## Pedagogical notes

- Part 1 ends unresolved so the learner must infer the risk before the short Part 2 reading.
- The dialogue models one instruction at a time, embedded questions, device functions, digital-safety modals and Unit 3 phrasal verbs.
- Speaker names are metadata and must never be spoken by the generated voices.
