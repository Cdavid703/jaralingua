# Intermediate English Course 2 - Unit 1 Listening Scripts

## Nora's Voice Note After the Saturday Table
File: `unit-1/noras-voice-note-after-the-saturday-table.mp3`

Nora: Hi, I just got home from Mr. Okafor's Saturday dinner, and I have to tell you about it. Mr. Okafor is the neighbor who lives across the hall. He seemed a bit serious at first, but he is actually the person who helps everyone in the building meet each other.

Nora: The biggest surprise was Iris, who was in my evening class years ago. She is an old classmate that I lost contact with when I changed jobs. We got back in touch tonight, and it felt completely natural.

Nora: I also met Gabriel, a photographer who recently moved into the building. He is the new contact that I talked to most during dinner. We hit it off because we both enjoy collecting stories about cities and people. He was kind of quiet at first, but once we started talking about photography, he became really enthusiastic.

Nora: So one dinner changed my social circle. I reconnected with someone from my past, got to know a new neighbor, and found two people I genuinely get on well with. Iris and I are meeting for coffee next week, and Gabriel and I promised to keep in touch.
