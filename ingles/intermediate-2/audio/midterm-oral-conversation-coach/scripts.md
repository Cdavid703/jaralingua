# Intermediate Course 2 Midterm Oral Conversation Coach — audio script

Canonical ElevenLabs source. Mia speaks in warm, natural General American English. Do not read headings, file names, or labels aloud.

### `turn-1-warm-hello.mp3`

Hi! I can't believe it has been so long. How have you been?

### `reaction-1-warm-hello.mp3`

I'm really happy to see you too. I've been busy, but I often thought about our school days.

### `turn-2-catching-up.mp3`

What have you been doing lately?

### `reaction-2-catching-up.mp3`

That's lovely. It is strange how quickly time passes, but some friendships still feel familiar.

### `turn-3-common-friend.mp3`

Valentina is unsure whether to stay with her partner after a serious lie. What do you think she should do?

### `reaction-3-common-friend.mp3`

Exactly. She feels hurt and confused, and we all want to support her without making the decision for her.

### `turn-4-second-conditional.mp3`

If you were in Valentina's situation, what would you do?

### `reaction-4-second-conditional.mp3`

I understand. It would be a painful decision, but thinking about trust and respect is important.

### `turn-5-your-dilemma.mp3`

Would you rather discuss an imaginary relationship dilemma or a general decision?

### `reaction-5-your-dilemma.mp3`

That makes sense. You can practice thoughtful advice without sharing anything personal.

### `turn-6-give-advice.mp3`

I have one too. I like someone, but I am afraid that a relationship could affect our friendship. What would you advise me to do?

### `reaction-6-give-advice.mp3`

That is helpful advice. I think I need to be honest and take things slowly instead of imagining the worst outcome.

### `turn-7-dating-question.mp3`

What question would you like to ask me about dating or relationships before we go?

### `reaction-7-dating-close.mp3`

For me, honesty, respect and good communication matter most. I'm glad we talked. Let's not lose touch again.

### `closing.mp3`

Thank you for talking with me. You started naturally, shared ideas, gave advice and asked a real question. Keep practicing until the conversation feels like your own.
