# Intermediate English Course 2 - Unit 2 Listening Scripts

## The Call Before Midnight
File: `the-call-before-midnight.mp3`

Salome: Mr. Vega, sorry to call this late, but I have to answer before midnight. A foundation offered me a full scholarship to study urban design in another city. I've dreamed of doing this for years, and I hope I can use what I learn to improve public spaces.

Mr. Vega: That sounds important. What is making the decision difficult?

Salome: I promised to lead our neighborhood library project next month. If I accepted the scholarship, I would have to leave before the project begins. The team is counting on me.

Salome: I feel torn. This could be a dream come true, but I don't want the neighborhood team to feel abandoned.

Mr. Vega: So you're at a crossroads. Which part worries you most?

Mr. Vega: Your concern shows responsibility, but it doesn't mean you must reject the opportunity.

Salome: Making the same mistake twice. Two years ago, I turned down an internship. I regret refusing it so quickly, and I wish I had asked whether the dates were flexible.

Mr. Vega: If I were you, I'd think this offer over before saying no. You could call the foundation and explain your commitment. They might work out a later starting date.

Salome: And if they say no?

Mr. Vega: Then weigh the consequences. Which decision could you live with? A responsible choice begins with all the facts.

Salome: Wait. It's eleven fifteen, and they just sent another email.

Mr. Vega: Open it.

Salome: The first sentence... it changes everything.
