Language noticing

How the speakers explain their ideas

A possibility

Why this language?

Might leaves room for other explanations. Being quiet does not prove boredom.

A strong deduction

Why this language?

Sam gives evidence: Maya has worked since six. Must expresses a deduction here, not an obligation.

Feeling and cause

Why this language?

Confused describes Maya’s feeling. Confusing describes the instructions that caused it.

A strong negative deduction

Why this language?

Sam rules out a literal report about cups asking for a holiday. Maya confirms that it is satire.

A contrast

Why this language?

Although connects two contrasting ideas: a funny poster and tiring work.

A friendly action

Why this language?

The idiom means help people start a conversation and feel comfortable.
