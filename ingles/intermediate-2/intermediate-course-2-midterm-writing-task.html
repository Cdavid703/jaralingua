COURSE: Intermediate Course 2 - MIDTERM WRITING TASK (20%)

“Catharsis for planning”

TYPE OF TASK: email

AUDIENCE: best friend abroad

PURPOSE: summarize regrets, wishes, hopes, plans, or dreams.

PROMPT: Recently, you have been experiencing a tough situation at work or with your family and you feel that you need to express your feelings. Write an email to your best friend who lives abroad. Explain what you would have done differently and what you wish or plan for your future so that you can start making life better.

INSTRUCTIONS: In your email you must,
  1. Describe your wishes, hopes, regrets, plans, or dreams.
  2. Write approximately 180 words.
  3. End your letter on an optimistic note.
  4. Spend 50 minutes on this task.

Language focus: make your meaning clear with Unit 2 language such as I wish I had…, I should have…, I hope to…, I would like to…, or My goal is to…. Use your own ideas; this is not a model to copy.
