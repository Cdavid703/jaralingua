Pronunciation focus

Make the important contrasts audible

screen · freezing · crashesStart screen with /skr/, finish freezing with /ng/, and keep two syllables in crashes.
could_youConnect final /d/ to the /y/ sound. The link often sounds like /dzh/ without losing either word.
a-VAIL-a-bleKeep four light syllables and place the strongest stress on VAIL.
hook_up · look_upLink the final consonant directly to the following vowel; do not leave a gap.
i-DEN-ti-ty · se-CU-ri-tyKeep the middle stress clear so the long words remain easy to recognize.
sus-PI-cious · SEN-si-tiveContrast the stress positions, then reduce mustn't without adding a syllable.
