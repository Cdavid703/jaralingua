Practice Lab · Unit 5 · Pair speaking

Behind the Picture

What could be happening?
Look closely, share an idea and listen to another view.

10 pictures4 conversation steps2 minutes per round
Choose a pictureHow to play

Two partners · three pictures per session

One picture. More than one explanation.

Look together for 15 seconds. Partner A describes, explains and gives a clue. Partner B offers another idea and a clue. Partner A responds. Swap who starts in the next round.

Use evidence, not assumptionsDifferent ideas can fit the same picture. Use might or could when you are not sure. Both partners speak.
Useful language · more ways to speak

Explain

More options

Use must or can’t only when strong clues support your deduction. A facial expression alone does not tell you exactly how someone feels.
