The task you are preparing for

An email to a best friend abroad

Imagine that a difficult work or family situation has made you reflect. Write to your best friend abroad, explain the choices you regret, share what you wish or hope for, and describe how you plan to make life better.

  • Use a clear From, To and Subject structure.
  • Write approximately 180 words.
  • Include regrets, wishes, hopes, plans or dreams.
  • End your email on an optimistic note.
Preparation, not evaluation.

Visual writing guide

Choose real ideas before you write

1. Reflect and look forward

Name one situation that was difficult. Then decide what you wish you had done differently and what you hope to do next.

2. Write to a real person

Address your best friend abroad. Explain your feelings clearly, then leave them with a positive and believable closing.

Writing checklist

  • Clear subject
  • Difficult situation explained
  • Regret pattern included
  • Hope, wish, dream or goal included
  • Next step explained
  • Optimistic ending
  • Close to 180 words

Useful connectors

However, because of this, as a result, from now on, in the future, even though, I believe that.

Use them to connect ideas; do not add them if they do not fit your meaning.
