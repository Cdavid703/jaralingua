How to play

See it, match it, say it

1Teams take turns choosing two numbered cards. Every revealed card shows a picture and its name.
2Find two identical pictures. A mismatch turns back over and passes the turn to the other team.
3Listen, say the word and read the example. The teacher awards the point; the team plays again.

Speaking challenge

Say it in context

Read the word and its model sentence. Then make your own sentence about a film or song.

The teacher decides when the response is ready. Game points do not affect course grades.
