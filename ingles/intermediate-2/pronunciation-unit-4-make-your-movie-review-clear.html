Pronunciation focus

Make your review easy to follow

I've_already · haven't_seenKeep contractions compact and join them to the next word.
watched · releasedBoth endings finish with /t/. Do not add another syllable.
uh-RIJ-uh-nuhl · SE-quelGive original its second-syllable stress; stress the first syllable of sequel.
GRIP-ping · con-VIN-cingMake the stressed syllables clear when explaining your opinion.
CATCH-y · SOUND-trackStress the first syllable and keep the final sounds clear.
check_it_out · edge_of_my_seatLink each expression in a smooth group; make OUT and SEAT strong.
