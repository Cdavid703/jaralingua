Yesterday’s Picture Challenge

Look closely. Listen to your teacher. Talk about yesterday with was and were.

Your speaking goal

Describe the picture as yesterday

Use Unit 1 weather vocabulary with was, were, wasn’t and weren’t. Give a short, clear answer: one or two sentences.

  1. Your teacher turns over a card.
  2. The roulette chooses a student for question 1.
  3. Another student answers question 2.
  4. After both pictures in a pair, compare the differences together.

Pronunciation matters: make was/wasn’t and were/weren’t sound different. Speak clearly enough for your classmates to hear.

How to answer

Teacher: Was it cold yesterday?
Student: No, it wasn’t. It was warm.

Teacher: Were the windows open?
Student: Yes, they were.
Quick language support

Was it…? Yes, it was. / No, it wasn’t.
Were they…? Yes, they were. / No, they weren’t.
Were there…? Yes, there were. / No, there weren’t.
