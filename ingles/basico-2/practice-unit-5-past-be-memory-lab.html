Past be: Memory Grammar Lab

Activity objective

Match the subject, the past form, and the meaning.

Use was / were for past states: where someone was, how someone felt, or what a place was like. This practice is not about did or action verbs.

First, identify the subject

I / he / she / it → was. You / we / they → were.

Then, locate the time

Each sentence refers to a finished moment: yesterday, last weekend, or last summer.

Read all three choices. One option is correct for both grammar and meaning.
