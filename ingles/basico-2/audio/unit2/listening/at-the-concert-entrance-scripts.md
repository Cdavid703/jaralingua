# Basic English 2 - Unit 2 Audio Listening Scripts

## At the Concert Entrance
File: `at-the-concert-entrance-listening.mp3`

Sofia: We are finally at the concert entrance. This blue jacket was a good idea.

Lucas: Yes, it looks great, and it matches your jeans. Wait, I just received a message from Noah.

Sofia: Is he here already?

Lucas: Not exactly. He says he is at the wrong entrance. He is standing near a coffee stand with two large posters behind him.

Sofia: That entrance is across the street. I can see those posters from here.

Lucas: Good. He says he is wearing a gray sweater and black pants.

Sofia: Perfect. I am wearing this blue jacket, so he can find us too.

Lucas: I am telling him, "Look for Sofia. She is wearing a blue jacket and jeans."

Sofia: Tell him we are near the ticket line, not near the doors.

Lucas: Done. He says the line at his entrance is moving slowly.

Sofia: Our line is moving fast. These tickets are for this entrance, right?

Lucas: Yes, these tickets are correct. We just need Noah to cross the street.

Sofia: Maybe he should not buy coffee now. The concert starts in ten minutes.

Lucas: He says, "I am coming now." He is walking across the street.

Sofia: I see him. Is that Noah next to the coffee stand?

Lucas: Yes, that's him. He is waving.

Sofia: Great. The jacket, the message, and the tickets all helped.

Lucas: And now we are ready for the concert.
