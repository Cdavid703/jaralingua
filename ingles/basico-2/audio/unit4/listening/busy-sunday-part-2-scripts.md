## A Busy Sunday: Part 2
File: `busy-sunday-part-2-listening.mp3`

Daniel: Thanks for inviting me, Valeria. I had a surprising Sunday.

Valeria: I know. My aunt called early this morning. She needed help at her community garden near San Pedro.

Daniel: I thought we went on a trip.

Valeria: We did, but it was a short trip. Two volunteers called off their visit, so my aunt needed extra hands.

Daniel: What did we do first?

Valeria: First, we picked up two boxes at the station café. They had gloves, seeds, and water bottles. Then we took the bus.

Daniel: Did the garden look nice?

Valeria: Yes. We cleaned the tables, carried the boxes, and planted flowers. My aunt prepared lunch for everyone.

Daniel: That sounds busy.

Valeria: It was. We were on the go all morning, but the volunteers were friendly. After lunch, we visited a small market and bought fruit for the ride home.

Daniel: When did we get home?

Valeria: At about five. You helped me carry the last boxes, and then we called it a night early.

Daniel: I was tired, but I enjoyed the day.

Valeria: Me too. Your help made a difference.
