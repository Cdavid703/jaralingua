# Basic English 2 - Unit 1 Audio Listening Scripts

## Changing Plans Because of the Weather
File: `weather-plan-change-listening.mp3`

Emma: Hi, Daniel. I am waiting near the park entrance. Are you coming?

Daniel: Hi, Emma. I am checking the weather right now. It is cloudy, and the wind is getting stronger.

Emma: Really? People are still walking in the park, but some families are leaving now.

Daniel: I see the same thing on the weather app. It says rain is starting in ten minutes.

Emma: Oh no. We are meeting up with Laura for the outdoor movie.

Daniel: I know, but look at the sky. It is getting very dark.

Emma: The food trucks are packing up too. Maybe they know something.

Daniel: Exactly. And listen. It is starting to rain here.

Emma: Here too. It is not pouring yet, but it is getting cold and windy.

Daniel: Then I think we should call off the outdoor movie plan.

Emma: I agree. Rain or shine sounds brave, but not today.

Daniel: Good. We are changing the plan. Let us meet up at the cafe across from the park.

Emma: Is Laura waiting there?

Daniel: Yes, she is sitting inside already. She is saving a table for us.

Emma: Perfect. I am putting on my jacket and going inside now.

Daniel: Great. Bring your umbrella. It is starting to pour.

Emma: Okay. See you at the cafe in five minutes.

Daniel: See you there.
