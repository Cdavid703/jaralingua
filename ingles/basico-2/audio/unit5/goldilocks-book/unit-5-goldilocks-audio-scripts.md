# Basic English Course 2 - Unit 5 Goldilocks Book audio

Each vocabulary card has one individual ElevenLabs model. The story is split by page so the interactive book can read the visible page or the complete story in sequence.

## cottage

File: `vocab-cottage.mp3`

Cottage. A small house, often in the country. The bears were in a cottage.

## forest

File: `vocab-forest.mp3`

Forest. A large area with many trees. Goldilocks was walking in the forest.

## porridge

File: `vocab-porridge.mp3`

Porridge. A soft hot food made with oats. The porridge was too hot.

## bowl

File: `vocab-bowl.mp3`

Bowl. A round container for food. Three bowls were on the table.

## empty

File: `vocab-empty.mp3`

Empty. With no people or things inside. The cottage was empty.

## chair

File: `vocab-chair.mp3`

Chair. A piece of furniture for sitting. The small chair was just right.

## soft

File: `vocab-soft.mp3`

Soft. Not hard; comfortable to touch or sit on. The medium chair was too soft.

## hard

File: `vocab-hard.mp3`

Hard. Not soft; difficult or uncomfortable to sit on. The big chair was too hard.

## asleep

File: `vocab-asleep.mp3`

Asleep. Sleeping, not awake. Goldilocks was asleep in the small bed.

## scared

File: `vocab-scared.mp3`

Scared. Afraid of something. Goldilocks was scared when the bears came home.

## page 1

File: `story-page-01.mp3`

Once upon a time, three bears were in a little cottage in the forest. Papa Bear was big. Mama Bear was kind. Baby Bear was small. On a sunny morning, their porridge was too hot. "Let's walk in the forest," said Mama Bear. The three bears were happy to leave the cottage for a few minutes.

## page 2

File: `story-page-02.mp3`

The cottage was quiet and empty. The three bowls were on the table, and the three chairs were near the fireplace. The bears were away in the forest.

## page 3

File: `story-page-03.mp3`

At the same time, Goldilocks was walking in the forest. She was hungry and a little tired. Soon, she saw the little cottage. The door was open. "Is anybody here?" she asked. There was no answer, so she walked inside.

## page 4

File: `story-page-04.mp3`

Inside, three bowls of porridge were on the table. The first bowl was too hot. The second bowl was too cold. The small bowl was just right, so Goldilocks ate it all.

## page 5

File: `story-page-05.mp3`

Then she saw three chairs. The big chair was too hard. The medium chair was too soft. The small chair was just right. After that, Goldilocks was very sleepy. Upstairs, she found three beds. The small bed was just right, and she fell asleep.

## page 6 narrator

File: `story-page-06-narrator.mp3`

Later, the bears were back. They were surprised to see the open door, the empty small bowl, and the little chair in the room.

## Mama Bear

File: `story-mama-bear.mp3`

## Baby Bear

## page 6 ending

File: `story-page-06-ending.mp3`

Goldilocks woke up. She was scared, so she ran home. After that day, she was careful when a house was empty.
