## Choosing the Right Place

Nora: Have you seen the two weekend options?

Eli: Yes. The first one is Valeria, a historic capital. Its old center is ancient, and the main museum looks impressive.

Nora: I like historic places, but is it very touristy?

Eli: It can be. The central streets are crowded at noon, but they are quiet early in the morning.

Nora: That sounds interesting. What about the second option?

Eli: Lake Miran is more remote. It is a small traditional village beside a scenic lake and mountains.

Nora: Is it peaceful?

Eli: Very. The lakefront is spacious, and the guide says the village is quiet after dinner.

Nora: I usually prefer modern cities, but I need a calm weekend.

Eli: Valeria has modern cafés and a famous tower. Lake Miran has short walks and no busy schedule.

Nora: Which place is easier to reach?

Eli: The train to Valeria takes two hours. The bus to Lake Miran takes four.

Nora: Hmm. I want to compare the guide again before we decide.

Eli: Good idea. Let's read the final details tonight.
