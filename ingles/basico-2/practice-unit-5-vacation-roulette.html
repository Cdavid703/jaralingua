My Last Vacation

Speaking roulette · Oral preparation for the Final Writing Task

Open the wheels

Prepare your four answers

You have 15 minutes to prepare your answers orally.

Think about the same vacation for all four answers. Rehearse speaking; use a few keywords if helpful. Your teacher will spin one wheel to choose a student and another to choose a question.

04 · Activities

What did you do there?

Speaking support

Answer in complete sentences. Use past forms such as went, traveled, visited, stayed, ate, was and were. Speak clearly and pay attention to pronunciation.

Place: Last…, I went to…   Time: I went in… / …ago.   Company: I traveled with… / I traveled alone.   Activities: First, I… Then, I…

Listen to your classmates. Feedback is oral; this activity has no score or submission.
