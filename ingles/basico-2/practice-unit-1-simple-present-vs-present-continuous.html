Quick grammar reminder

Before you choose

Simple Present

Routines and facts

Use the base verb with I, you, we, they. Add -s or -es with he, she, it.

Present Continuous

Actions happening now

Use am / is / are + the -ing form of the main verb.

Spelling

Watch the -ing form

Examples: make -> making, run -> running, study -> studying.
