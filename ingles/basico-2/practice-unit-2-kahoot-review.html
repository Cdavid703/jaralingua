Practice Lab · Unit 2 · Activity 09

Kahoot Review: Shopping Experiences

Play a fast formative review before the writing task. This Kahoot reviews Unit 2 language without revealing the official exam content.

Open Kahoot Back to Unit 2 Practice Lab

Goal

What students will review

Students will recognize and use Unit 2 language for shopping situations, clothing descriptions, prices, sizes, and simple writing support.

Class setup

Before starting

  1. The teacher opens the Kahoot link and projects the game PIN.
  2. Students join with their real first name and last name initial.
  3. The teacher reminds students to answer in English only.

Game rules

During the game

  1. Read each question carefully before choosing an answer.
  2. After each question, correct the mistake orally as a class.
  3. Use the review to prepare your own sentences, not to memorize answers.

Language focus

Unit 2 checklist

Clothes and accessoriesjacket, jeans, boots, scarf, belt, sunglasses.
Demonstrativesthis, that, these, those for distance and number.
Prices and sizesHow much is it? It costs... I am a medium.
Shopping languagetry on, on sale, cost an arm and a leg, fit well.

Teacher note

After the game

Choose three difficult questions and ask students to explain why the correct answer works. Then ask them to write two original Unit 2 sentences.
