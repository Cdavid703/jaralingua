Practice Lab · Unit 2 · Activity 09

Kahoot Review: Shopping Experiences

Play a fast teacher-led Kahoot review before the writing task. This activity reviews Unit 2 language without revealing the official exam content.

KahootShopping reviewTeacher-ledEnglish only

01

Activity Goal

Review Unit 2 before writing

Students will review clothes, accessories, prices, sizes, shopping phrases, demonstratives, phrasal verbs, idioms, simple present, present continuous, and useful sentence writing.

02

Teacher Setup

Before starting

  1. Open the Kahoot link and project the game PIN.
  2. Ask students to join with their real first name and last name initial.
  3. Tell students that the activity is a review, not the official exam.
  4. Keep the PDF answer bank for teacher use only.
03

Student Instructions

During the game

  1. Read each question carefully before choosing an answer.
  2. Answer in English only.
  3. After each question, listen to the correction.
  4. Use the review to prepare your own sentences, not to memorize answers.
04

Language Checklist

What this Kahoot reviews

Clothes and accessories

jacket, jeans, boots, scarf, belt, sunglasses, sweater, dress.

Demonstratives

this, that, these, and those for distance and number.

Prices and sizes

How much is it? It costs... I am a medium. Do you have this in medium?

Shopping expressions

try on, on sale, cost an arm and a leg, fit well, What is it like?

05

Kahoot Colors

Answer options

Students will see four answer colors in Kahoot. The correct answers are mixed to avoid a visible pattern.

A · Red
B · Blue
C · Yellow
D · Green
06

Teacher Follow-up

After the game

  1. Choose three difficult questions.
  2. Ask students to explain why the correct answer works.
  3. Ask students to write two original Unit 2 sentences.
  4. Connect the sentences to a short shopping or city-description paragraph.
