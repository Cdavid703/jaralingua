Quick review

Remember the form

Short adjective

the + -est

big → the biggest
cold → the coldest
old → the oldest

Long adjective

the most + adjective

the most famous
the most beautiful
the most interesting

Group pattern

one of the most...

Use a plural noun:
one of the most famous landmarks
