Practice Lab · Unit 2 · Activity 07

How Much Is It?

The teacher shows a clothing item with a price. Students answer aloud using dollars, Colombian pesos, numbers, and shopping vocabulary.

PricesNumbersClothesElevenLabs example
Start price practice Review Unit 2 Practice Lab
Activity Objective

Students will ask and answer prices naturally using How much is...?, How much are...?, It's..., and They're... with clothing, accessories, dollars, and Colombian pesos.

Teacher Flow

How to run the activity

Show one price card, ask the question aloud, let the student answer, then reveal the model answer only after the attempt.

1

Show the item

The card displays a clothing item or accessory with a price in dollars or Colombian pesos.

2

Ask the price

Use How much is...? for singular items and How much are...? for plural items.

3

Student answers

The student says the price aloud using It's or They're.

Language Model

Natural question and answer patterns

Singular item

How much is the jacket?
It's forty-eight dollars.
It's one hundred eighty-five thousand Colombian pesos.

Plural item

How much are the sunglasses?
They're twenty-eight dollars.
They're ninety thousand Colombian pesos.

Currency clue

🇺🇸 USD = dollars
🇨🇴 COP = Colombian pesos
