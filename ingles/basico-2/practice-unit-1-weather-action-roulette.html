Practice Lab · Unit 1 · Activity 09

Weather Action Roulette

The teacher reveals a hidden weather card, the roulette selects a student, and the student speaks freely using present continuous.

SpeakingWeatherPresent continuousTeacher-led
Start activity Back to Practice Lab
Activity Objective

Students will produce a natural sentence about an activity happening now, based on a weather condition selected by the teacher. The goal is oral fluency, not a written task.

01

Teacher Flow

How the activity works

The card stays face down until the teacher reveals it. After the weather is visible, spin the student roulette. The selected student says at least one sentence with present continuous. The teacher gives oral feedback only.

1

Reveal weather

Open one hidden card so the class can see the weather situation.

2

Spin student

The roulette chooses who speaks for the round.

3

Speak freely

The student says one or more natural sentences using present continuous.

02

Language Reminder

Useful model answer

Students can use the model first, then change the clothing, activity, or place according to the weather card.

It's + weather. I'm + verb-ing. He's / She's + verb-ing. We're + verb-ing. They're + verb-ing. now / right now / today
Model answer

It's sunny, so I'm wearing sunglasses and I'm going for a walk.

It's cloudy, so I'm wearing a jacket and I'm walking to a cafe.

It's raining, so I'm carrying an umbrella and I'm staying inside.
