Audio Listening: Changing Plans Because of the Weather

Listen to Emma and Daniel change an outdoor plan because the weather is getting worse.

Audio listeningWeatherPresent continuous10 questions
Start activity Back to Practice Lab Review Unit 1
Practice Goal

Understand a short audio conversation about weather, current actions, phrasal verbs, and a change of plan without video support.

01

Before You Listen

Language for the Audio

Review the key language before listening. Focus on weather, actions happening now, and expressions for changing plans.

Weather: cloudy, windy, raining, pouring.

Actions now: checking, waiting, leaving, packing up.

Phrasal verbs / idioms: go out, meet up, call off, rain or shine.

02

Audio Practice

Conversation: Changing Plans Because of the Weather

Listen once for the main idea. Listen again for the weather, the actions, and the final plan.

03

Listening Focus

Three Things to Notice

1. Weather changeWhat happens to the weather from the beginning to the end?
2. Current actionsWho is waiting, checking, leaving, packing up, or sitting inside?
3. Final planWhat do Emma and Daniel decide to do instead?
04

Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

Choose the best answer. These questions check meaning from the audio, not isolated translation.

1. What is Daniel doing at the beginning?

2. Where is Emma waiting?

3. What is the weather like at first?

4. What is happening in the park?

5. What are the food trucks doing?

6. Why does Daniel want to call off the outdoor movie plan?

7. What does “call off” mean in the conversation?

8. Where are they meeting instead?

9. What is Laura doing?

10. Why is “rain or shine” not a good idea today?
