Visual writing guide

Use the images to plan your ideas

Do not copy a model. Use the visual prompts to decide what to say about your real city, weather, activities, sports, study, and work responsibilities.

1. City and weather

Write your city name. Describe common weather and what the weather is like these days: sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy, hot, cold, or stormy.

2. Activities and responsibilities

Use present continuous to say what you are doing now: studying English, working, doing homework, playing sports, helping at home, or preparing projects.

Small model
