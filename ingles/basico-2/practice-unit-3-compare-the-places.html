Grammar guide

Review before comparing

Comparative 1

Short adjectives: -er

quiet → quieter
old → older
tall → taller

Comparative 2

Long adjectives: more + adjective

more crowded
more peaceful
more impressive

Superlative 1

Short adjectives: the -est

the quietest
the tallest
the oldest

Superlative 2

Long adjectives: the most + adjective

the most scenic
the most touristy
the most impressive
