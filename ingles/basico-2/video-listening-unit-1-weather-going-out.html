Practice Goal

Understand a short conversation about weather, current actions, and a plan change using Unit 1 language.

01

Before You Watch

Language for the Video

Review the key language before watching. Listen especially for weather words, actions happening now, and expressions for changing a plan.

Weather: cloudy, windy, raining, pouring.

Actions now: checking, looking outside, warming up, going inside.

Phrasal verbs / plan changes: calling off, meeting up, changing the plan, staying dry.

Listen for these present continuous forms

Do not try to understand every word on the first watch. First, identify who is doing each action now.

I am checking People are walking They are playing The coach is calling off Players are warming up We are changing

03

Watch Strategy

Three Things to Notice

1. WeatherWhat is the weather like at the beginning and at the end?
2. Current actionsWho is checking, walking, playing, warming up, or waiting?
3. Plan changeWhere are Emma and Daniel going instead?
04

Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

Choose the best answer. These questions check weather, present continuous, and the speakers' plan.

1. What is Daniel doing at the beginning?

2. What is the weather like at first?

3. What does Emma see outside?

4. Where is it raining?

5. Are they still playing soccer?

6. What is the coach doing?

7. What are some players doing before going inside?

8. What plan are Emma and Daniel changing?

9. Where are they going instead?

10. Why does Daniel tell Emma to bring an umbrella?
