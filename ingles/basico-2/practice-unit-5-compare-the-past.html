Practice Lab · Unit 5 · Activity 08

Compare the Past

Look, decide and correct. Compare two past scenes with was and were.

Class speaking12 cardsComparatives
Open the cardsReview was / were

Your speaking goal

Say yes or no. Then compare.

Compare size, weather, prices and places from the past. Give one or two clear sentences, using a comparative with than.

  1. Look at both pictures and their labels.
  2. The roulette chooses a classmate.
  3. Listen to one question. Answer and give the full comparison.
  4. If the statement is wrong, correct it.

Pronunciation matters: pronounce was, were, wasn’t, weren’t and the comparative clearly. Your teacher will give oral feedback.

Language support

Yes, it was. … was … than …
No, it wasn’t. … was … than …
Yes, they were. … were … than …
No, they weren’t. … were … than …

Word bank: bigger, smaller, taller, shorter, longer, colder, warmer, cheaper, more expensive, busier, quieter, more crowded, cleaner.

Clean: without dirt or litter. Crowded: full of people. Quiet: with little noise.
