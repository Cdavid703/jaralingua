Unit 1

Going Out

Weather, sports, current activities, and simple social plans.

Open bank
How to read these cards: The Spanish meaning is approximate. The explanation tells students when the expression is natural in English.
PV Phrasal verbs
phrasal verb

go out

salir

Leave home for a social activity, meal, event, or plan. We are going out tonight.

phrasal verb

hang out

pasar el rato

Spend relaxed time with friends without a formal plan. I am hanging out with my friends at the park.

phrasal verb

meet up

encontrarse / reunirse

Meet someone at an agreed place or time. We are meeting up at the mall.

phrasal verb

come over

venir a la casa de alguien

Go to someone's home or current place. Do you want to come over tonight?

phrasal verb

stay in

quedarse en casa

Not go out; remain at home. It is raining, so we are staying in.

phrasal verb

warm up

calentar / hacer calentamiento

Prepare the body before sports or exercise. The players are warming up before the game.

phrasal verb

work out

hacer ejercicio / entrenar

Exercise physically, usually for health or fitness. She works out three times a week.

phrasal verb

join in

unirse / participar

Start participating in an activity that other people are already doing. Can I join in the soccer game?

phrasal verb

turn up

aparecer / llegar

Arrive at a place, sometimes unexpectedly or late. He turned up late for the match.

phrasal verb

call off

cancelar

Cancel a plan, event, match, or activity. They called off the game because of the rain.

ID Idioms and common expressions
expression

It's pouring.

está lloviendo muy fuerte

Use it for heavy rain. We cannot go out. It's pouring.

expression

It's freezing.

hace muchísimo frío

Use it for extreme cold. Wear a jacket. It's freezing outside.

expression

It's boiling.

hace muchísimo calor

Use it for extreme heat. Let's stay inside. It's boiling today.

idiom

under the weather

sentirse mal / estar indispuesto

This means a person does not feel well. It is not literal. I am not going out because I feel under the weather.

idiom

rain or shine

sin importar el clima

Use it when something happens no matter the weather. We play soccer every Saturday, rain or shine.

idiom

a fair-weather friend

amigo solo en los buenos momentos

A person who is present only when things are easy. This is less basic, so it can be treated as extra vocabulary. He is a fair-weather friend.

expression

get some fresh air

salir a tomar aire

Go outside for a short time to feel better or relax. Let's go for a walk and get some fresh air.

idiom

hit the road

salir / ponerse en camino

Start leaving or begin a trip. It is late. Let's hit the road.

idiom

have a blast

divertirse mucho

Have a very good time. We had a blast at the game.

expression

be on the same team

estar del mismo lado / trabajar juntos

Share the same goal or cooperate. We need to help each other. We are on the same team.
