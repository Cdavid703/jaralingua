idiom

It's raining cats and dogs.

está lloviendo a cántaros

Informal and playful. Use it for very heavy rain, not for formal weather reports. We cannot play outside; it's raining cats and dogs.

idiom

take a rain check

dejarlo para otra ocasión

Use it to politely postpone a plan. It does not need to be raining. Can we take a rain check and meet tomorrow?

idiom

Use it when a plan will happen no matter the situation. We are going to practice soccer, come rain or shine.

idiom

under the weather

This means a person does not feel well. It is not literal. I am not going out because I feel under the weather.

idiom

Shopping and Clothes

Clothes, accessories, prices, sizes, fit, and natural store language.

Open bank
How to read these cards: Phrasal verbs often change meaning when the particle changes. The Spanish meaning is approximate, and the example shows the natural shopping context.
PV Phrasal verbs
phrasal verb

try on

probarse ropa

Put clothes on before buying them to check size, color, or comfort. Can I try on this jacket?

phrasal verb

Place clothing on your body. Put on your sweater. It is cold outside.

Click to listen
phrasal verb

Remove clothing from your body. Please take off your hat inside.

phrasal verb

pick out

Choose one item from several options. I need to pick out a dress for the concert.

Click to listen
phrasal verb

Try to find a specific product, size, or color. I am looking for a black jacket.

Click to listen
phrasal verb

Click to listen
