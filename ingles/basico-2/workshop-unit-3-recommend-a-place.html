Practice Lab · Unit 3 · Activity 09

Recommend a Place

Work in a group of three. Create a short, visual travel recommendation using the language from Unit 3.

Groups of 33–4 minutesOral workshopNot graded
Start planningBack to Practice Lab

Activity objective

Make a clear recommendation together

Your group will recommend one city, country, landmark, or natural place. Speak for three to four minutes and support your ideas with a simple visual presentation.

1 place2 accurate adjectives1 comparative1 superlative1 recommendation

Three speakers

Share the presentation fairly

1

Place guide

Introduce the place and country. Give two interesting features using Unit 3 adjectives.

Speak for: 45–60 seconds.

2

Comparison guide

Compare the place with another place. Explain who might enjoy it and why.

Speak for: 45–60 seconds.

3

Recommendation guide

Use one superlative, give a reason, and close the recommendation clearly.

Speak for: 45–60 seconds.

Your visual presentation

Build four simple slides with images

Use three or four clear photos. Do not fill the slides with long paragraphs: the images help your audience understand while you speak.

PHOTO 1 · PLACE

Slide 1 · Where is it?

Name the place and country. Add one attractive image.

PHOTOS 2–3 · DETAILS

Slide 2 · What is it like?

Show two features and describe them with strong adjectives.

PHOTO 4 · COMPARE

Slide 3 · Compare

Place two images or names side by side for your comparative sentence.

PHOTO 5 · RECOMMEND

Slide 4 · Choose it

End with your superlative and the reason for your recommendation.

Language bank

Use language from Unit 3

Describe: “Cartagena is a warm, lively, and historic city.”
Compare: “Cartagena is warmer than Bogotá.”
Use a superlative: “It is one of the most beautiful cities in Colombia.”
Recommend: “We think it is the best place for a warm weekend because it is beautiful and interesting.”

Teacher model

Listen to the flow of one group

Student 1

Place guide

“Hello. Our place is Cartagena, in Colombia. It is a beautiful and warm city. It is famous for its beaches and colorful streets.”

Student 2

Comparison guide

“Cartagena is warmer than Bogotá, and it is more popular with tourists than many small cities. It is a good place for people who like the beach.”

Student 3

Recommendation guide

“For us, Cartagena is one of the most beautiful cities in Colombia. We think it is the best place for a warm weekend because it is beautiful, popular, and interesting. Thank you.”

Presentation route

Prepare, rehearse, and share

Choose

Agree on one place and collect three to five useful photos.

Build

Create four visual slides. Write short prompts, not a complete script.

Rehearse

Each person practises their part aloud and helps the next speaker continue naturally.

Present

Show the images and speak to the class as one group for three to four minutes.

Before you present

Final group checklist

  • We chose one clear place.
  • Our slides include clear images.
  • We used at least two Unit 3 adjectives.
  • We used one comparative accurately.
  • We used one superlative accurately.
  • We gave a clear reason for our recommendation.
  • All three students speak.
  • We are ready to speak, not read.

Teacher note: This is an oral workshop, not an assessed deliverable. Give oral feedback after each group, especially on clarity, comparatives, superlatives, and pronunciation.
