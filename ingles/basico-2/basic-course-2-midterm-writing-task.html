COURSE: Basic Course 2- PP – MIDTERM WRITING TASK (20%)

"City, weather and activities these days"

TYPE OF TASK: magazine post

AUDIENCE: Angel magazine readers

PURPOSE: inform readers about your city, the weather, and current activities

PROMPT: Angel magazine has an interactive section where readers write about their cities and the activities they are doing these days. Write a post about your city, its common weather, the activities you are doing now, the sports you are playing, and your current study or work responsibilities.

INSTRUCTIONS: In your post you must,
  1. Write a clear post title.
  2. Start with: Hi there,
  3. Mention your city and the common weather there.
  4. Describe what you are doing these days, including activities, sports, and study or work responsibilities.
  5. End with a friendly question or invitation for the readers.
  6. Finish with: Take care,
  7. Spend 50 minutes on this task.
  8. Write at least 100 words.
Remember: Use simple present for general facts and routines, and present continuous for actions happening now or around these days.
