BASIC COURSE 2 – FINAL WRITING TASK (20%)

My last vacation — individual exam · Type: a postcard · Audience: classmates

Think about your last vacation. Write a postcard to your classmates about where you went, when you went, who you traveled with, what you did, and one funny or embarrassing moment. Show what made your vacation special.

1. Plan your story Choose a destination and organize your ideas.

2. Write your postcard Sign in with your own account and complete all three sections yourself.

3. Review and send Read the complete postcard. Confirm your review, then send your own work.

Instructions, timing and assessment

You have 48 hours (2 days) from the moment you start. Organize your ideas, plan, write, revise, and submit within this time. The timer belongs to your personal exam and continues when you close the page. Saved work is kept when you return.

Write about 120 words for your complete postcard. Use simple past to narrate and was/were to describe. Use negatives when your story needs them. Connect events with first, then, after that or finally. Phrasal verbs, idioms and comparisons are optional.

Use I for your own experiences and we when you describe things you did with other people. Create an original story; there is no completed model to copy.

Your teacher assesses content, organization, vocabulary, structure and mechanics: 10 points each, 50 in total, converted to a grade out of 5. Each student receives an individual grade.

Choose a picture as inspiration; it does not need to show the exact destination. The three sections help you organize a single postcard. You write, revise and submit all of them. This exam accepts only one student per new submission.
