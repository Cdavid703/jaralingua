BASIC COURSE 2 – FINAL WRITING TASK (20%)

My last vacation — group adaptation · Type: a postcard · Audience: classmates

You traveled together during your last vacation. Write a postcard to your classmates about where you went, when you went, who you traveled with, what you did, and one funny or embarrassing moment. Show what made your vacation special.

1. Plan together Choose a destination and agree on your story.

2. Everyone writes Each person signs in and writes their assigned part.

3. Review and send Read the complete postcard. Everyone confirms; one member sends it.

Instructions, timing and assessment

You have 50 minutes from the moment your team starts: 5 minutes to get organized, 8 to plan, 20 to write, 10 to revise and 7 to check and submit. The timer is shared by your team.

Write about 120 words for the whole postcard, not 120 per person. Use simple past to narrate and was/were to describe. Use negatives when your story needs them. Connect events with first, then, after that or finally. Phrasal verbs, idioms and comparisons are optional.

Use we for shared experiences. You can mention a teammate by name in your anecdote. Create an original story; there is no completed model to copy.

Your teacher assesses content, organization, vocabulary, structure and mechanics: 10 points each, 50 in total, converted to a grade out of 5. Each student receives an individual grade.

Choose a picture as inspiration; it does not need to show the exact destination. Your teacher assigns the three writing parts. In a pair, one student writes two parts. Discuss corrections together; each author updates their own part.
