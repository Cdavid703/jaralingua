Basic English Course 2

Unit 2: Shopping Experiences

Learn how to talk about clothes, accessories, prices, sizes, preferences, needs, and simple store situations in natural everyday English.

Unit Objective

By the end of this unit, students will understand how to name common clothes and accessories, ask and answer prices, describe style and fit, express likes and needs, and use practical shopping expressions in stores or shopping areas.

02 Items that usually work in plural
What we will learn: We will learn why some shopping words often need plural language in English.

Singular items

Use a or this/that with one item.

a shirt this jacket that scarf

Common plural items

Use these/those or plural verbs with items that normally come in pairs or plural form.

these jeans those shoes these sunglasses

This jacket is comfortable.

These jeans are expensive.

Those shoes are elegant.

04 Sizes and fit
What we will learn: We will learn how to talk about clothing size and whether something fits well.

Size language

What size are you? I am a small / medium / large. Do you have this in medium?

Fit language

It fits well. It is too big. It is too small. It is tight / loose.

Usage note

Fit means the size is correct for the body. Comfortable means it feels good when you wear it.

05 Wear, try on, and buy
What we will learn: We will learn the difference between three essential shopping verbs.
shopping verb

wear

usar / llevar puesto

Use clothes on your body. I wear jeans on weekends.

phrasal verb

try on

probarse ropa

Put clothes on before buying them. Can I try on this jacket?

shopping verb

buy

comprar

Pay money for something. I need to buy new shoes.

Topic cluster 4

Store Language and Shopping Expressions

This final cluster gives students the phrases they need for basic interaction and description.

Open topics
09 Store interaction phrases
What we will learn: We will learn short, polite phrases that people use in stores.
store phrase

Can I help you?

A shop assistant offers help. Can I help you?

store phrase

I'm just looking, thanks.

A polite way to say you do not need help now. I'm just looking, thanks.

store phrase

Do you have this in another size?

Use it when the item is too big or too small. Do you have this in medium?

store phrase

Do you have this in another color?

Use it when you like the item but want a different color. Do you have this in black?

store phrase

Where are the fitting rooms?

Ask where you can try on clothes. Where are the fitting rooms?

store phrase

I'll take it.

Use it when you decide to buy something. It fits well. I'll take it.

10 What is it like?
What we will learn: We will learn how to ask for a description, not a preference.

Ask for description

What is it like? What is that perfume like? What is the store like?

Answer with adjectives

It is fresh and sweet. It is big and modern. It is comfortable but expensive.

Common confusion

In What is it like?, the word like means "similar to / description." It does not mean "gustar."

11 Store routines and reviews
What we will learn: We will learn how to describe a store, a shopping area, and normal shopping routines.

Store information

The store opens at 7 a.m. The store closes at 9 p.m. The mall has many clothing stores.

Shopping routines

I usually go to the mall. People buy clothes at this store. This store sells shoes and accessories.
12 Real shopping expressions and idioms
What we will learn: We will learn common shopping expressions that sound natural in real contexts.
idiom

shop till you drop

comprar hasta cansarse

Informal and playful. Use it when someone shops a lot. On Saturday, we are going to shop till we drop.

expression

window shopping

mirar vitrinas sin comprar

Looking at products without planning to buy them. We are just window shopping today.

expression

on sale

en oferta

Use it when the price is lower than usual. This jacket is on sale.

expression

a good deal

una buena oferta

A good price for the value. These shoes are a good deal.

idiom

cost an arm and a leg

costar un ojo de la cara

Informal. Use it for something very expensive. That perfume costs an arm and a leg.

expression

in style / out of style

de moda / pasado de moda

Use it to talk about fashion. White sneakers are in style.

13

Language Model

Store conversation model for reading and analysis

Assistant: Can I help you?

Customer: Yes, please. I need to buy a jacket.

Assistant: What size are you?

Customer: I am a medium. Do you have this jacket in black?

Assistant: Yes, we do. It is on sale today.

Customer: Great. How much does it cost?

Assistant: It costs eighty thousand pesos.

Customer: It fits well. I'll take it.

Unit Summary

What this explanation covers

Vocabulary

Students recognize clothes, accessories, styles, sizes, fit words, and shopping adjectives.

Shopping patterns

Students use practical patterns such as How much is...?, How much does it cost?, I like to wear..., and I need to buy....

Store interaction

Students understand phrases such as I'm just looking, Can I try it on?, Do you have this in another size?, and I'll take it.

Expressions

Students meet real shopping expressions such as shop till you drop, window shopping, on sale, and a good deal.
