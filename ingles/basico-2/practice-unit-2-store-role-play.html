Unit 2 · Speaking production

Store Role-Play: Shopping Conversation & Mini Presentation

Students work in pairs or groups of three to simulate a real clothing-store conversation using Unit 2 vocabulary, prices, sizes, phrasal verbs, and idioms.

Pairs or triosStore conversationPhrasal verbsIdioms
Start planning Back to Practice Lab

Objective

What students will do

Students will create and present a short shopping conversation. One student enters a clothing store as the customer, another student acts as the shop assistant, and a third student may join as a friend or second customer.

2

Pair option

Student A is the customer. Student B is the shop assistant.

3

Trio option

Student A is the customer, Student B is the shop assistant, and Student C is a friend or second customer.

0%

Class production

This is a speaking practice activity. The teacher gives oral feedback during presentations.

Methodology

How the activity works

Required language

Every group must include

01

Clothes and accessories

Use Unit 2 vocabulary such as jacket, jeans, dress, sweater, shoes, scarf, sunglasses, watch, or hat.

02

Size, color, and fit

Ask or answer about size, color, or fit: small, medium, large, extra large, black, blue, too big, too small, or it fits well.

03

Prices

Ask and answer a price question: How much is it? or How much does it cost?

04

One phrasal verb

Use at least one: try on, put on, take off, pick out, look for, pay for, check out, sell out, go with, or dress up.

05

One idiom or expression

Use at least one: fit like a glove, cost an arm and a leg, break the bank, worth every penny, for a steal, on sale, or a good deal.

06

Clear pronunciation

Speak clearly and do not read too fast. Give special attention to the phrasal verb and the idiom or expression.

Board preparation

What students write before presenting

Phrasal verb: try on

Sentence: Can I try on this jacket?

Idiom / expression: fit like a glove

Sentence: This jacket fits like a glove.

The board is not extra decoration. It helps the class notice the required language before hearing it in the conversation.

Teacher model

Example conversation to perform with one student

Use this model at the end of the explanation so students understand the activity before they prepare their own version.

Phrasal verb: try on

Sentence: Can I try on this jacket?

Idiom / expression: fit like a glove

Sentence: This jacket fits like a glove.

Teacher / Shop assistant: Good afternoon. Can I help you?

Student / Customer: Yes, please. I am looking for a black jacket.

Teacher / Shop assistant: Of course. What size are you?

Student / Customer: I am a medium. Do you have this jacket in medium?

Teacher / Shop assistant: Yes, we do. Here you are.

Student / Customer: Great. Can I try on this jacket?

Teacher / Shop assistant: Sure. The fitting rooms are over there.

Student / Customer: It fits like a glove. How much does it cost?

Teacher / Shop assistant: It costs eighty thousand pesos. It is on sale today.

Student / Customer: That is a good deal. I will take it.

Teacher / Shop assistant: Excellent. You can pay for it at the front desk.
