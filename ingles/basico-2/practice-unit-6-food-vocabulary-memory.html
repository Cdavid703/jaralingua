Food Vocabulary Memory

Find food pairs, listen, and say each word aloud.

Start practiceReview the rule
Activity objective: Recognize and pronounce Unit 6 foods through a two-team memory game.
How to play

Two teams take turns revealing two cards. A matching pair earns one point. Listen to the word, repeat it aloud, and press “We repeated it — continue”. A match keeps the turn; a miss passes it to the other team. The team with more pairs wins.
