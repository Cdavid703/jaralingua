Speaking toolkit · Look here before you turn away

Give clues in this order

  1. Place: It is a city / an ancient place. There is… There are…
  2. Weather & clothes: It looks sunny. People are wearing…
  3. Details: I can see… On the left… In the background…
  4. Activities: You can visit… You can take…

Use your own words. If you do not know a detail, say “I’m not sure.” Do not invent facts.

Ask for useful clues

  • Is it a city? Is it near the sea?
  • Is there a river? Are there mountains?
  • What is the weather like in the picture?
  • What are the people wearing?
  • What can I do there? Can I take a boat?
  • Can you repeat that, please?
  • I think it is… Is it…?

Answer in short, complete sentences: “Yes, there is.” “No, there aren’t.” “You can…”

For the final oral task, practise describing places, exchanging information and using can / can’t. After the reveal, ask: “Would you like to visit? Why?”

Student 1 · Describe aloud

“It is a big city. In the picture, there is a tall metal tower and a green park. It looks sunny. I can see people walking and taking photos. There is some bread and fruit on a picnic blanket. You can have a picnic and visit the tower.”

Your route: general place → things you see → weather → people → activities. Keep the city and tower names secret!

Student 2 · Ask, then guess

Guesser: “Is there a famous tower?”
Describer: “Yes, there is.”

Guesser: “What can I do there?”
Describer: “You can walk in the park and take photos.”

Guesser: “Can I swim in the park?”
Describer: “No, you can’t.”

Guesser: “I think I know! Is it…?”

Make your guess aloud. Use Reveal destination to check.
