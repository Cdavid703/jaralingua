Unit 1

Going Out

Weather, sports, current activities, and natural going-out expressions.

Open unit
Unit objective: By the end of this unit, students will understand how to describe weather, recognize actions happening now, identify the difference between habits and current activities, and use common going-out phrasal verbs and idioms in context.
01Weather and Going OutWeather words, expressions, and decisions. 02Present ContinuousActions happening now. 03Sports and Activitiesplay, go, do, work out. 04Phrasal Verbs and IdiomsNatural language for plans.

Shopping Experiences

Shopping language, preferences, prices, sizes, and customer-service situations.

Open unit
Unit objective: By the end of this unit, students will understand how to name common clothes and accessories, ask and answer prices, describe style and fit, express likes and needs, and use practical shopping expressions in stores or shopping areas.

Unit 3

Around the World

Countries, places, travel descriptions, and simple comparisons.

Open unit
Unit objective: By the end of this unit, students will understand how to name countries and nationalities, describe famous places, compare cities or countries, and use basic superlatives to talk about important places around the world.
01Countries and NationalitiesCountry names, nationality adjectives, and origin. 02Famous PlacesLandmarks, nature, and travel adjectives. 03Simple ComparisonsComparative adjectives and than. 04SuperlativesThe biggest, the most famous, one of the most.

Unit 4

Busy Lives

Simple past for last night and last week: time expressions, questions, negatives, and busy-life stories.

Open unit
Unit objective: By the end of this unit, students will understand how to describe completed recent activities, use regular and common irregular past forms, ask and answer questions with did, make negatives with didn't, and organize a short story about a busy night or week.
01Past Time and ActionsLast night, yesterday, last week, and completed events. 02Past Verb FormsRegular spelling, irregular verbs, and -ed sound awareness. 03Past Questions and NegativesDid, short answers, and didn't + base verb. 04Busy-Life MessagesShort stories, diary entries, voicemail, and WhatsApp replies.
