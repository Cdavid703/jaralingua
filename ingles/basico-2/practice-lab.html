Unit 1

Going Out

Weather, current activities, sports, and plans. These activities focus on weather recognition, -ing spelling rules, simple present vs. present continuous, video listening, audio listening, reading comprehension, pronunciation follow-up, a private conversation coach, and teacher-led oral production.

Weather Call-Out Cards

Students look at weather cards, answer What's the weather like?, reveal the model sentence, and listen to the ElevenLabs audio.

02 Grammar Practice

-ing Spelling Rules

Students choose the correct -ing form, identify spelling rules, and check common mistakes through multiple-choice questions.

Open activity
03 Grammar Workshop

Simple Present vs. Present Continuous

Students transform routine sentences into current actions and current actions into simple present routines using A, B, and C choices.

Open activity
04 Video Listening

Going Out or Staying In?

Students watch a weather conversation, identify present continuous forms, and answer ten comprehension questions.

Open activity
05 Audio Listening

Changing Plans Because of the Weather

Students listen to a weather conversation without video support, identify present continuous forms, and answer ten comprehension questions.

A Rainy Afternoon Plan

Open activity

Unit 2

Shopping Experiences

Clothes, accessories, prices, sizes, and shopping language. This folder includes vocabulary recognition, visual demonstratives, reading, audio listening, pronunciation, conversation coaching, price speaking, pair role-play production, and a Kahoot review.

Open folder
01 Vocabulary Activity

Shopping Memory Vocabulary

Two teams find clothing and accessory pairs, listen to the ElevenLabs model, and say the item aloud before the teacher awards the point.

Open activity
02 Grammar Practice

This, That, These, Those

Students inspect fifteen shopping images, enlarge each scene, and choose the correct A, B, or C sentence according to distance and number.

Open activity
03 Reading

A Jacket for the Concert

Students read part one of a shopping story, follow clothes, size, fit, price, and accessory details, and answer ten A, B, C comprehension questions.

Open activity
04 Audio Listening

At the Concert Entrance

Students listen to part two of Sofia and Lucas's story, follow a phone message, identify clothes and locations, and answer ten A, B, C questions.

Open activity
05 Pronunciation

Shopping and Concert Pronunciation

Students listen to seven professional models, record six guided sections plus the final challenge, and send a 0% pronunciation report to the teacher.

Open activity
06 Conversation Coach

Shopping with Ella

Students have a coherent five-turn conversation that starts naturally, moves into an after-class plan, and ends with clothing, shopping, prices, and their own questions.

Open activity
07 Speaking / Prices

How Much Is It?

Students look at clothing price cards, hear ElevenLabs examples, and answer aloud with dollars, Colombian pesos, numbers, It's, and They're.

Open activity
08 Pair / Trio Speaking

Store Role-Play

Pairs or trios prepare a clothing-store conversation, write their phrasal verb and idiom on the board, and present with clear pronunciation.

Open activity
09 Kahoot / Review

Kahoot Review

Unit 4

Busy Lives

Simple past practice combines regular-verb spelling, the three sounds of -ed, guided pronunciation with teacher follow-up at 0%, an oral classroom roulette about last weekend, and a connected reading-and-listening story.

Open folder
01Grammar Practice

Regular -ed: Spelling and Sound Lab

Students choose accurate past forms and identify /t/, /d/, or /ɪd/ with individual ElevenLabs models, checked feedback, and unlimited retries.

Open activity
02Pronunciation · 0% Follow-up

Regular -ed Pronunciation Studio

Students listen to each ElevenLabs verb model, record the /t/, /d/, and /ɪd/ sound groups, receive word-level feedback, and send a 0% report to the teacher.

Open activity
03Oral classroom activity

Last Weekend Roulette

The teacher starts a 15-minute preparation timer, loads the active Course 2 roster, and spins the roulette. Each student tells a weekend story using four connectors, a Unit 4 phrasal verb, and an idiomatic expression.

06Conversation Coach · 0%
