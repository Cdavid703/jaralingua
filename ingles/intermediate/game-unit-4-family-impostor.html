Practice Lab - Vocabulary Speaking Game

The Vocabulary Impostor

A live hidden-role conversation game. The teacher chooses Unit 2, Unit 3, or Unit 4 vocabulary. Most students receive one secret word or expression; the impostor receives no item and must infer it from the discussion.
