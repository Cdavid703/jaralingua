    Teacher flow

    How to run it

    • Create one room.
    • Choose and launch a scenario.
    • Read answers aloud.
    • Nominate 2-4 finalists.
    • Open voting and reveal the class decision.

    Student language

    What students produce

    • A real decision, not one word.
    • At least one Unit 6 marker.
    • A reason or trade-off.
    • A clear final action.

    Pedagogical product

    Final class result

    • The class compares ideas.
    • The teacher can discuss grammar live.
    • The winning answer becomes the class recommendation.
    • No gradebook pressure.
