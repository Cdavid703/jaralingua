Intermediate English Course 1

Course Overview

Welcome to the Intermediate English Course Overview. In this section, you will find the main units for this level. Each unit is designed to help you speak, understand, and express ideas with more detail and confidence.

You will continue developing your English through topics related to personality, behavior, stereotypes, dreams, hopes, wishes, unusual life experiences, human and natural wonders, world records, family responsibilities, respectful disagreement, memories, food quantities, and cultural dishes.

Course units

Choose a topic to study

These units help you move from basic communication to more complete and natural English. You will practice describing people, talking about habits, expressing wishes, sharing dreams, discussing unusual experiences, comparing wonders around the world, negotiating family responsibilities respectfully, and explaining food through quantities, ingredients, culture, and opinions.

Unit 1

The Way We Are – Stereotypes and Behavior

Explore how people describe personality, behavior, and stereotypes. Learn how to talk about the way people act, think, and interact in different situations.

  • Describing personality and behavior
  • Talking about stereotypes carefully
  • Explaining how people usually act

Family Problems and Memories

Use fictional family scenarios to talk about obligations, permission limits, respectful disagreement, mediation, and memories from the past.
