What is an idiom?

An idiom is a special expression where the meaning is different from the literal meaning of the individual words. Idioms are common in real conversations, movies, songs, interviews, and informal writing.

Why learn idioms?

Idioms help you understand English beyond grammar rules. They show emotion, attitude, and cultural meaning. However, idioms should be used carefully: they are usually more common in informal or semi-formal communication.
