Practice Lab · Creative Writing

Create a Superhero or Supervillain

In this group activity, students create an original superhero or supervillain, write a character description, and present their ideas to the class.

At the end, the class can compare the characters and analyze which hero could defeat another hero, and which villain could defeat which hero, according to the abilities described in the writing models.

Read instructions Open group tool

Instructions for students

Work in groups of up to four students. Your group must create one original character: a superhero or a supervillain. Then, write a short writing model describing the character’s personality, habits, and powers.

Character creation

Give your character a name and decide if it is a hero or a villain.

Writing goal

Write a paragraph using personality adjectives, habits, and abilities.

Important detail

Mention the powers clearly so the system can later compare who could defeat whom.

How to do the activity

Follow these steps carefully before presenting your character.

1

Choose your character type

Decide if your group will create a superhero or a supervillain.

2

Create a name

Give your character an original name that matches its personality or powers.

3

Write the paragraph

Describe the character’s personality, habits, and abilities. Include clear sentences such as “He can control fire” or “She can fly quickly.”

4

Present to the class

Share your character and explain why it is powerful, helpful, dangerous, brave, selfish, or special.

Useful language

Students can use these expressions in their writing models.

Personality and habits

  • He is brave, helpful, patient, and responsible.
  • She is selfish, rude, careless, and annoying.
  • He always helps people.
  • She usually works alone.
  • He never gives up.
  • She is always causing problems.

Powers and abilities

  • He can control fire.
  • She can fly very fast.
  • He can become invisible.
  • She uses technology and armor.
  • He controls water and ice.
  • She uses light energy to protect people.

Example for students

This is a model example. Students should create a different and original character.

Writing model

The Helpful Guardian

The Helpful Guardian is a kind and responsible superhero. He is very helpful, patient, brave, and considerate. He always protects people in danger, and he usually listens carefully before making decisions. He can create light shields, he can fly quickly, and he uses light energy to heal small injuries. He never abandons the community because he wants to help everyone.
Image prompt

Prompt example

Create an original superhero called The Helpful Guardian. He is kind, responsible, patient, brave, and considerate. He has the power to create glowing light shields, fly quickly, and use light energy to protect people and heal small injuries. He wears a blue and teal superhero suit with warm yellow details, a modern cape, and a friendly heroic expression. Show him in a clean modern city while protecting citizens. The style should be colorful, classroom appropriate, semi-flat vector illustration, bright background, no text, no logo, no watermark.
