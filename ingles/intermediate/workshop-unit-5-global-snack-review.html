Practice Lab - Unit 5 Writing

Global Snack Review

Review a snack or small dish with ingredients, quantities, taste evidence, rating, and a respectful cultural comparison.

WritingReviewFood adjectivesCulture
Start reviewBack to Unit 5 folder
01

Purpose

Write a Review with Evidence

Ingredients

Name what the snack is made of or made with. Include at least one quantity phrase, not only a list of foods.

Taste and texture

Use precise adjectives: crispy, creamy, spicy, salty, sweet, filling, light, fresh, crunchy, warm, soft.

Culture

Compare respectfully. Explain when people eat it, what it reminds you of, or what similar food exists elsewhere.

02

Review route

Move from Description to Recommendation

Step 1

Name it

Identify the snack, country, region, family habit, restaurant, or food fair connection.

Step 2

Describe it

Include ingredients, quantities, taste, texture, temperature, and how it is served.

Step 3

Interpret it

Explain why it matters culturally or socially without making stereotypes about people.

Step 4

Recommend it

Give a rating and a realistic reason connected to evidence from the snack.

Open language bank
  • Ingredients: It is made with corn flour, cheese, a little oil, and some fresh sauce.
  • Quantity: One small serving is enough as a light snack, but two pieces can feel filling.
  • Taste: It is salty and warm, with a crispy outside and a soft inside.
  • Culture: It reminds me of street food because people can eat it quickly and share it easily.
  • Comparison: It is similar to another snack in my culture because both are practical and affordable.
  • Recommendation: I would give it 4 out of 5 because it is tasty, clear to describe, and easy to serve in class.
03

Models

What a Strong Review Sounds Like

Ingredient sentence

This snack is made with corn flour, cheese, and a little oil.

Opinion sentence

I would give it 4 out of 5 because it is crispy outside, soft inside, and not too heavy.

Culture sentence

It reminds me of street food because it is simple, warm, and easy to share.

Open full model review

I reviewed a small cheese arepa connected to Colombian street food. It is made with corn flour, cheese, a little butter, and some fresh sauce. I give it 4 out of 5 because it is warm, salty, crispy on the outside, and soft inside. One small arepa is enough for a light snack, but two pieces can feel filling. Culturally, it reminds me of food people buy on the way to school, work, or a family visit. I would compare it with other simple snacks because it is affordable, practical, and easy to share. I recommend it for a class food fair because students can describe its ingredients, texture, quantity, and cultural meaning clearly.

04

Teacher review rubric

How the Review Will Be Read

Evidence

Specific food details

The review mentions real ingredients, at least one quantity, and clear taste or texture evidence.

Opinion

Rating with reasons

The score is supported by concrete details, not only by “I like it” or “it is delicious.”

Culture

Respectful connection

The student explains a place, habit, memory, or comparison without stereotypes or empty generalizations.

Product

Class-ready review

The final text is organized enough for the teacher to read aloud and discuss in class.
