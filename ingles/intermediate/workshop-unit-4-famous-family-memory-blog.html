Practice Lab - Unit 4 Writing

Family Memory Blog Workshop

Write a short blog post about a fictional or public family memory. Compare past habits with present routines and finish with a realistic reflection.

Writingused toPast vs presentPeer feedback
Start writingBack to Unit 4 folder
01

Input

Model Blog Paragraph

The Riveras used to spend every Sunday afternoon in the kitchen. Rosa used to tell short stories, and the children used to ask questions before dinner. Now everyone is busier, so the family cannot copy the old tradition exactly. Their new agreement is simple: one phone-free half hour, one shared task, and one story at dinner. The memory is useful because it helps them design a better present.
02

Planning

Build Your Blog

Choose the family

Use the Riveras, another fictional family, or a public/famous family. Do not expose private personal stories.

Past habit

Write three sentences with used to or didn't use to.

Present contrast

Explain what is different now using now, today, or these days.

Reflection

Finish with one sentence explaining what the memory teaches.

03

Product

Blog Requirements

Length

120 to 160 words.

Language

3 uses of used to, 2 present contrast sentences, and 1 obligation or agreement sentence.

Tone

Respectful, fictional, and reflective. Avoid blaming real relatives.

Feedback

A partner checks clarity, grammar, and whether the final reflection makes sense.

Final check

Read it aloud before submitting

Use the pronunciation page to practice sentence stress and connected speech in your family memory paragraph.
