Practice Lab - Unit 4 Writing

Family Memory Blog Workshop

Write a short blog post about a fictional or public family memory. Compare past habits with present routines and finish with a realistic reflection.

01

Input

Model Blog Paragraph

Past habit: The Riveras used to spend every Sunday afternoon in the kitchen. Details: Rosa used to tell short stories, and the children used to ask questions before dinner. Present contrast: Now everyone is busier, so the family cannot copy the old tradition exactly. Agreement: Their new agreement is simple: one phone-free half hour, one shared task, and one story at dinner. Reflection: The memory is useful because it helps them design a better present.
02

Planning

Build Your Blog

1. Choose a safe family

Use the Riveras, another fictional family, or a public/famous family. Do not expose private personal stories from your real family.

The Morales family used to visit their grandmother every Saturday.

2. Name the old routine

Start with one repeated habit. Use used to for something that happened before but is different now.

They used to cook soup together and listen to old music.

3. Add two memory details

Give small concrete details: place, people, food, objects, music, time, or a repeated question.

The children used to write the menu on a small yellow card.

4. Explain the present contrast

Show what changed. Use now, today, or these days.

Now the children have homework, and the adults answer work messages.

5. Finish with an agreement

The blog should not only feel nostalgic. It must end with a realistic decision for today.

Today, they have to protect thirty minutes for dinner without phones.

6. Add a reflection

Explain what the memory teaches. This final sentence gives the blog a purpose.

The memory teaches them that a tradition can change and still matter.
03

Examples

Useful Sentence Models

Opening

  • The family used to...
  • When the children were younger, they used to...
  • Every Sunday, they used to...

Past habit

  • They did not use to eat in separate rooms.
  • Rosa used to tell one story before dinner.
  • Carlos used to play music while Ana cooked.

Present contrast

  • Now everyone has a different schedule.
  • These days, phones interrupt the conversation.
  • Today, they cannot repeat the old routine exactly.

Agreement

  • They have to share one task.
  • They agreed to put phones away for thirty minutes.
  • The new rule has to be fair for adults and teenagers.

Reflection

  • The memory helps them choose a better habit.
  • The past was not perfect, but it gave them a useful idea.
  • A tradition can change without disappearing.

Common mistake

Incorrect: They used to cooked together.

Correct: They used to cook together.

04

Product

Blog Requirements

Length

120 to 160 words is ideal. The submission system accepts 90 to 220 words.

Language

Use used to or didn't use to, present contrast language, and one obligation or agreement sentence.

Tone

Respectful, fictional, and reflective. Avoid blaming real relatives.

Class reading

The teacher will be able to read submitted responses and may choose examples to discuss in class.
