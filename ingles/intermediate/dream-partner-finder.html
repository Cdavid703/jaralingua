Practice Lab · Wishes, Hopes and Dreams

Dream Partner Finder

In this activity, students share a wish, hope, or dream and explain what they need to achieve it. Then, the tool connects students with possible dream partners.

A dream partner is someone with a similar dream, a similar need, or a different dream that could still create a meaningful connection.

Read instructions Open tool

Instructions for students

Each student must write one wish, hope, or dream in English. Then, they must explain what they need to achieve it.

Write your dream

Use expressions such as “I wish...”, “I hope...”, “I dream of...”, or “I have always wanted to...”

Explain your needs

Say what you need to make your dream possible: money, time, English, discipline, information, support, practice, or advice.

Find a connection

The tool will connect students based on similar dreams, similar needs, or possible support between different dreams.

Student examples

These examples show how students can write their information clearly.

Example 1

Name: Laura
Dream: I dream of studying in another country.
Need: I need to improve my English, save money, and learn about scholarships.

Example 2

Name: Daniel
Hope: I hope I can travel to Europe one day.
Need: I need to save money, get a passport, and practice English.
