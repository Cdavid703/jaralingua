Choose the Right Function

Obligation

have to explains a responsibility: Leo has to set the table.

Limit

won't let explains permission: Carlos won't let phones stay on the table.

Request

want someone to explains what one person expects: Ana wants Maya to help with salad.

Past habit

used to explains a repeated past situation: They used to eat earlier.
