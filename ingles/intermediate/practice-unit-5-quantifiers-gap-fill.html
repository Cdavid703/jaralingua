Quick Grammar Map

Read the sentence before choosing. The best answer depends on the noun and the sentence type, not only on the food word.

Countable plural

Use many for questions or large numbers, and a few for a small positive number.

many eggsa few tomatoes

Uncountable food

Use much in questions and negatives, and a little for a small useful amount.

much ricea little oil

Existence or absence

Use some in affirmative sentences, offers, and requests. Use any in questions and negatives.

some cheeseany bread?
