Each activity helps you practice intermediate English in a different way: grammar, speaking, personality descriptions, stereotypes and behavior, habits, hopes, dreams, classroom interaction, creative writing, and real communication.

01

Grammar Workshop: Adjectives vs. Manner Adverbs

Practice the difference between adjectives and manner adverbs. Learn how to describe what people are like and how they behave in different situations.

Open activity
02

Classroom Activity: The Stereotype Guessing Game

Use personality adjectives, behavior descriptions, and classroom interaction to guess characters, discuss stereotypes, and explain your ideas respectfully.

Open activity
03

Describing Stereotypes and Behavior

Describe stereotypes associated with different people and their behavior. Practice using adjectives, manner adverbs, and respectful language to explain what people are believed to be like, how they usually behave, and why generalizations can be unfair or incomplete.

Open activity
04

Personality Battle: Create a Superhero or Supervillain

Work in groups to create an original superhero or supervillain. Write a character description, describe personality traits and habits, and compare powers with your classmates.

Open activity
05

Dream Partner Finder

Insert students’ wishes, hopes, or dreams and what they need to achieve them. Then find dream partners based on similar goals, shared needs, or possible support.

Open activity
06

Listening: The Mirror Guardian

Listen to a professional A2+ superhero story about stereotypes, first impressions, and looking beyond labels. Then answer fifteen detailed comprehension questions with feedback.

Open activity
07

Reading: The Wall of Names

Continue The Mirror Guardian story with a 250-word A2+ reading about names, rumors, and the real stories behind quick labels. Includes illustrated story scenes and twelve comprehension questions.

Open activity
08

Guess Who Game: Professions Edition

Reuse the full roulette-based classroom game with profession portraits, yes/no questions, participation history, and vocabulary support.
