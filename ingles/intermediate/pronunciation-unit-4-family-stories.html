01

Focus

Four Guided Sections

1. Used to

Say used to smoothly: /yoostuh/ in connected speech.

We used to cook together on Sunday afternoons.

2. Have to

In fast speech, have to often sounds like /hafta/.

Everyone has to help for twenty minutes.

3. Want someone to

Keep the person clear, then connect to lightly.

Ana wants Leo to set the table before dinner.

4. Respectful disagreement

Stress the polite contrast: I understand, but...

I understand your point, but we need a fair schedule.
02

Final Challenge

Read the Complete Paragraph

The Riveras used to cook together every Sunday. Now everyone is busier, so they have to make a realistic plan. Ana wants everyone to help for twenty minutes, and Carlos won't let phones stay on the table. The new rule is simple, fair, and easier to follow.
