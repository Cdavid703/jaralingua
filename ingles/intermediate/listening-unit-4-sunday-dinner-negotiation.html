01

Input

Listen for Problems, Limits and Solutions

Professional audio

First listen without reading. Write the name of each person, one responsibility, one problem, and one suggested solution.

Strategy: Do not try to write every word. Listen for obligation phrases: has to, doesn't have to, won't let, wants someone to, and used to.

Useful mediation language

  • I understand your point, but...
  • Can we make a fair schedule?
  • What if everyone helps for twenty minutes?
  • Let's agree on one rule everyone can follow.
