Food Vocabulary Memory Game

Two teams reveal food cards, find matching pairs, listen to the model audio, and classify each food before earning the point.

How to play

  1. Team 1 chooses two hidden cards.
  2. If the foods match, the student listens to the model audio.
  3. The team chooses the correct grammar basket: countable, uncountable, plural, or serving.
  4. The student repeats the word or sentence, and the teacher confirms the point.
  5. If the cards do not match, the turn passes to the other team.
