Food Vocabulary Memory Game

Two teams reveal food cards, find matching pairs, listen to the model pronunciation, and say the food word clearly before earning the point.

12 pairs24 cardsTeam gameVocabularyPronunciation
Play gameReview Unit 5Practice Lab

How to play

  1. Team 1 chooses two hidden cards.
  2. If the foods match, the student listens to the model audio.
  3. The student repeats only the food word clearly.
  4. The teacher confirms the pronunciation to award the point.
  5. If the cards do not match, the turn passes to the other team.
