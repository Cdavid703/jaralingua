Grammar map

What the story checks

The activity is contextual. Students do not choose isolated words; they decide what the speaker means inside one complete narrative about plans, advice, conflicts, and final decisions.

Future intentionUse be going to when the plan already exists before the conversation.
Confirmed arrangementUse present continuous when the calendar or appointment is already fixed.
Decision nowUse will when the speaker decides or offers something during the conversation.
Advice and schedule languageUse should, ought to, could, fit in, free up, and key idioms naturally.
