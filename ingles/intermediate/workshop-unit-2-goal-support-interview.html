Practice Lab - Unit 2 Speaking

Goal Support Interview

Interview a classmate about a real or fictional goal, ask follow-up questions, and write a practical support plan using hopes, wishes, and future intentions.

SpeakingWritingHopes and wishesFollow-up questions

01

Purpose

From Dream to Support Plan

Not only matching

The goal is not to find someone with the same dream. The goal is to understand another person's goal and propose one useful support action.

Language

I hope to...I wish I could...I would like to...I am planning to...

Final product

Submit a short interview report. The teacher can read it in the follow-up submissions panel.

02

Interview Questions

Ask, Listen and Clarify

Goal

What do you hope to do in the next year?

Why is this goal important to you?

Obstacle

What makes this goal difficult?

What do you wish you had more of: time, money, confidence, practice, or support?

Evidence

What have you already done?

What skill or habit do you need to improve?

Support

How could a classmate help you?

What small action could you take this week?

03

Model

Short Report Example

Model paragraph

My partner hopes to improve her English speaking because she wants to feel more confident in job interviews. She wishes she had more opportunities to speak outside class. She has already practiced pronunciation, but she needs to organize her ideas faster. I can support her by asking two interview questions every week. Her first small action is to record a one-minute answer and listen to it again.
