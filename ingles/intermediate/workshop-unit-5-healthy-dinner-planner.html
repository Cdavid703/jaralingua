Healthy Dinner Planner

Design a balanced dinner someone could actually serve. Include quantities, ingredients, health reasons, and one cultural connection.

01

Purpose

Plan a Dinner Someone Could Actually Serve

Audience

Name the group and number of people. A plan for two people is different from a plan for six classmates.

Quantities

Use exact phrases: two cups of rice, a bag of carrots, six pieces of chicken, a little oil, a few strawberries.

Reason

Explain why the dinner is balanced, light, filling, affordable, colorful, or culturally meaningful.

02

Dinner design

Use the Plate Map

Balanced plate

The final dinner must include at least three food groups and one reason for each important choice.

Grainsrice, pasta, arepa, quinoa, bread
Proteinbeans, chicken, eggs, fish, lentils
Vegetableslettuce, carrots, tomatoes, spinach
Fresh sideavocado, fruit salad, yogurt, herbs

Quality checklist

  • State who the dinner is for and how many people will eat.
  • Include at least five ingredients with natural quantity phrases.
  • Explain why the dinner is healthy or balanced.
  • Connect the menu to Colombian food, another culture, or a family habit.
  • Show that the meal is realistic: not too expensive, too heavy, or too vague.
Open useful phrase bank
  • Audience: This dinner is for six classmates at an international food fair.
  • Ingredients: It is made with two cups of cooked rice, one bowl of beans, and some fresh vegetables.
  • Health: It is balanced because it includes grains, protein, vegetables, and a light dessert.
  • Quantity control: We use a little oil and a few pieces of fruit so the meal does not become too heavy.
  • Culture: It is inspired by Colombian home food, but it is presented as a modern dinner bowl.
  • Recommendation: I would serve this dinner because it is colorful, filling, affordable, and easy to share.
03

Model

What a Strong Plan Sounds Like

Ingredient sentence

Our dinner is made with two cups of rice, one can of beans, four pieces of chicken, some lettuce, and a little lime dressing.

Health sentence

It is balanced because it has grains, protein, vegetables, healthy fat, and a light dessert.

Culture sentence

It is inspired by Colombian home food, but we present it as a modern dinner bowl.

Open full model paragraph

Our dinner is for six classmates at an international food fair. The main dish is a Colombian-inspired rice bowl made with three cups of cooked rice, two cans of beans, six pieces of grilled chicken, some lettuce, two avocados, a little lime dressing, and a few pieces of mango. It is balanced because it has grains, protein, vegetables, healthy fat, and a light fruit dessert. It is inspired by Colombian home food, but it is presented as a modern dinner bowl. I would serve this meal because it is colorful, filling, affordable, and easy to share.

04

Teacher review rubric

How the Dinner Plan Will Be Read

Quantity language

Ingredients are measurable

The plan uses natural quantity phrases such as two cups of rice, one can of beans, some lettuce, a little oil, or a few pieces of fruit.

Balanced meal

The dinner makes sense

The meal includes more than one food group and explains why it is filling, light, healthy, affordable, or realistic for the group.

Cultural connection

The menu has a reason

The student connects the dinner to a family habit, Colombian food, another culture, a place, or a food memory without forcing the idea.

Clarity

The product is easy to read

The final paragraph is organized, specific, and ready for the teacher to read in class. It does not contain empty placeholders or vague food lists.
