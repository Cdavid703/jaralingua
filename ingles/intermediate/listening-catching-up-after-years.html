Catching Up After Years

Listen to two friends sharing recent life events, organizing their stories in a timeline, and asking thoughtful follow-up questions.

American English Two voices Timeline order 20 questions A2+ / B1
Start listening Written activity
01

Listening Task

Follow the Timeline

Maya and Ethan were close friends in high school, but they have not had a long conversation for years. Now they meet again and share what has happened in their lives.

Audio conversation

Two American English voices: Maya and Ethan.

Listening strategy: do not choose an answer because one word sounds familiar. Compare dates, reasons, feelings, and results before choosing.

02

Comprehension Check

Answer the 20 Questions

1. Why do Maya and Ethan meet at the cafe?

2. What was Maya's first big event after graduation?

3. How did Maya feel during her first month in Denver?

4. What helped Maya feel more connected in Denver?

5. What happened to Maya's company later?

6. What did Maya do after she moved back home?

7. Why did Ethan postpone one college semester?

8. Where did Ethan spend many long days during that period?

9. What did Ethan eventually realize about that difficult semester?

10. When did Ethan return to classes?

11. What recent goal has Ethan started working on?

12. Where does Maya work now?

13. What does Maya interview people about?

14. Which idea do both friends seem to share?

15. What do Maya and Ethan promise to do next?

16. Which follow-up question does Ethan ask Maya?

17. Why is the conversation a good model for the written activity?

18. What does Maya say a good catch-up conversation should not be?

19. What does Ethan say about listening to Maya's timeline?

20. What is the best summary of the conversation?
