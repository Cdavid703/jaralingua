Write for a real team

Imagine your group is preparing a shared food-fair table for six people.

  • Name the dish or food-fair table.
  • Write 5 to 7 sentences.
  • Use at least four target quantifiers.
  • Include one question and one negative sentence.
  • Give one reason for an amount.
