Section 2 - 25 points - recommended time: 40 minutes

Integrated writing

You are writing for an international food and culture magazine. Review one snack or dish and decide whether it is everyday food, special-occasion food, or both.

Include:
  • Name and cultural origin.
  • Ingredients with at least two clear quantity expressions.
  • Taste and texture evidence.
  • A comparison with another food.
  • Your experience, rating, and recommendation.

Use Unit 5 language such as some, any, much, many, a few, a little, made with, and Have you ever tried...?
