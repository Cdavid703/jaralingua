03

Product

Defend the Most Interesting Country

Use the chart to prepare a short recommendation. Your final product must include facts, superlatives, a comparison, and a reasoned conclusion.

Evidence

Use at least two measurements: meters high, kilometers long, years old, or square kilometers.

Language

Use at least four superlatives and one comparison with another country.

Conclusion

Finish with a clear recommendation: This country is the most interesting because...

Final paragraph or speaking notes

Product

Country competition debate

In groups, present your chart and defend your country as the most interesting country to visit. Use at least four superlatives, two measurement questions, and one comparison with another country.
