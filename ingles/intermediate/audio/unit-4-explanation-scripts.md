# Unit 4 Explanation Audio Models

## Unit 4 Intro
File: `unit-4-explanation/unit-4-intro.mp3`

Narrator: In this unit, you will learn how to talk about family responsibilities, limits, expectations, and memories in a respectful way. You will use a fictional family, so you can practice clearly without exposing private problems.

## Have To Model
File: `unit-4-explanation/have-to-model.mp3`

Narrator: Listen to the obligation model. Leo has to set the table. Maya has to finish her presentation. Carlos has to wash the dishes. After have to, use the base verb: set, finish, wash.

## Limits Model
File: `unit-4-explanation/wont-let-model.mp3`

Narrator: Listen to the limits model. Carlos won't let phones stay on the table. Ana won't let one person do all the work. After won't let, use the person and the base verb.

## Expectations Model
File: `unit-4-explanation/want-someone-to-model.mp3`

Narrator: Listen to the expectations model. Ana wants everyone to help. Maya wants Leo to set the table. Leo wants Maya to help him tomorrow. The person after wants is the person who should do the action.

## Used To Model
File: `unit-4-explanation/used-to-model.mp3`

Narrator: Listen to the past habits model. The Riveras used to cook together on Sundays. Rosa used to tell stories before dinner. They didn't use to order food very often. After didn't, say use to, not used to.

## Mediation Model
File: `unit-4-explanation/mediation-model.mp3`

Narrator: Listen to the mediation model. I understand your point, but we need a fair schedule. What if everyone helps for twenty minutes? Let's agree on one rule everyone can follow.
