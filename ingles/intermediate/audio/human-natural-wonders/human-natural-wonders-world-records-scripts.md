# Unit 3 Audio Scripts: Human and Natural Wonders / World Records

## Audio 1: Human and Natural Wonders
File: `intro-human-natural-wonders.mp3`

Today we are going to talk about amazing places around the world. Some places are natural wonders, like rivers, mountains, forests, and waterfalls. Other places are human wonders, like buildings, walls, monuments, and cities. In this unit, we will use superlatives to describe the most beautiful, the tallest, the longest, the biggest, and the best places in the world.

## Audio 2: Short Superlatives
File: `short-superlatives-est.mp3`

Listen and repeat. The tallest, the longest, the highest, the largest, the oldest, the fastest, the smallest. Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world. The Great Wall of China is one of the largest walls in the world. The Amazon is one of the longest rivers in the world.

## Audio 3: Long Superlatives
File: `long-superlatives-most.mp3`

Listen and repeat. The most beautiful, the most interesting, the most important, the most luxurious, the most dangerous, the most amazing, the most popular. Cano Cristales is one of the most beautiful rivers in Colombia. The Amazon is one of the most important natural wonders in the world. Cartagena is one of the most popular cities in Colombia.

## Audio 4: Irregular Superlatives
File: `irregular-superlatives.mp3`

Good, better, the best. Bad, worse, the worst. Far, farther, the farthest. Cartagena is the best place to visit during a long weekend. That was the worst trip of my life.

## Audio 5: Superlative Questions
File: `superlative-questions.mp3`

What is the most beautiful river in Colombia? What is the tallest mountain in the world? What is the best place to visit during a long weekend? What is the most famous human wonder in the world? What is the most interesting place you know?

## Audio 6: Measurements with How
File: `measurements-how-adjective.mp3`

How tall is the building? It is eight hundred twenty-eight meters tall. How high is the mountain? It is eight thousand eight hundred forty-nine meters high. How long is the river? It is six thousand four hundred kilometers long. How old is the monument? It is two thousand years old.

## Audio 7: Big Numbers
File: `big-numbers-world-records.mp3`

Eight hundred twenty-eight. One thousand two hundred. Two thousand three hundred. Six thousand four hundred. Eight thousand eight hundred forty-nine. One million.

## Audio 8: Cartagena Commercial Model
File: `cartagena-commercial-model.mp3`

Welcome to Cartagena, one of the most beautiful cities in Colombia. Cartagena is the best place to visit during a long weekend. You can visit the beach, walk through the Walled City, eat delicious food, and enjoy amazing views. You should visit Cartagena because it is exciting, colorful, and unforgettable.

## Audio 9: World Record Blog Model
File: `world-record-blog-model.mp3`

Today, the world needs new records to help people and the planet. One important record could be the largest recycling campaign in the world. Another record could be the cleanest city in the world. In my opinion, world records should help us create a better future.
