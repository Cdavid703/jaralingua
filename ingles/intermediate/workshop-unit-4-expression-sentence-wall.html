Activity design

How the Live Wall Works

  1. The teacher opens one expression. Only that idiom or phrasal verb is available for submissions.
  2. Students write one original sentence. The sentence must use the open expression and connect to Unit 4: family responsibilities, memories, negotiation, or conflict repair.
  3. The sentence appears on the wall. The wall refreshes automatically, so the teacher can read examples aloud in class.
  4. The class discusses clarity. The focus is communicative accuracy: meaning, context, and natural use.
