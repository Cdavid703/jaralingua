Listening focus

This activity trains students to hear why each future form is chosen inside a real planning problem.

  • be going to for prior plans and intentions.
  • present continuous for confirmed future arrangements.
  • should / could for advice and possible solutions.
  • will for decisions made during the conversation.
  • fit in, free up, put off and related idioms in context.
