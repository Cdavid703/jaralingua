Prompt: Listen to a short food-review program. Answer ten questions, and then write your own evidence-based food review. You may submit only once.

Section 1 - 25 points

Listening

Listen to the professional American English conversation as many times as you need. Choose the best answer for each question. The audio is available only while the exam is open.

Speed
Audio speed is set to 1x. Replay is available without a limit.

Section 2 - 25 points - recommended time: 40 minutes

Integrated writing

You are writing for an international food and culture magazine. Review one snack or dish and decide whether it is everyday food, special-occasion food, or both.

Include:
  • Name and cultural origin.
  • Ingredients with at least two clear quantity expressions.
  • Taste and texture evidence.
  • A comparison with another food.
  • Your experience, rating, and recommendation.

Use Unit 5 language such as some, any, much, many, a few, a little, made with, and Have you ever tried...?

Write a complete review. There is no word-count limit for submission.
