Common error bank

three rices

Use a container or measurement: three cups of rice. Rice is uncountable in this food context.

many water

Use much for uncountable questions, or a container phrase: a bottle of water.

a few oil

Use a little oil because oil is uncountable. A few is for countable plural nouns.

a bread

Use a slice of bread or a loaf of bread when you need to count the amount.
