Workshop Brief

Mission and Setup

Students use a real, drawn or imagined family photo to describe family members and answer classmates questions.

Time

50 minutes

Grouping

Individual gallery, then pairs

Main output

Family photo description card

Based on guide

Show and tell family picture; describe family members

  • Photo or drawing
  • Gallery cards
  • Sticky notes
  • Question bank
02

Task Cycle

Activity Rounds

Follow the rounds in order. The teacher can stop after any round for quick pronunciation or grammar feedback.

  1. Prepare the gallery cardStudents choose three people in the picture and write name, relation, adjective and one simple fact.
  2. Silent gallery walkClassmates walk, read, and leave one yes/no question on a sticky note.
  3. Pair interviewStudents answer the questions orally with a partner.
  4. Famous families linkPairs compare one real family and one famous family using simple be-verb sentences.
  5. Teacher feedbackThe teacher reviews family vocabulary and third-person be forms.
