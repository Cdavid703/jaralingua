Free-Time and TV Habits Fair

Indoor or outdoor wall

Make two lists: staying-in activities and going-out activities. Vote for the three most common activities in class.

2

TV habits survey

Ask classmates: "What kind of TV shows do you like?", "How often do you watch TV?", and "Do you watch alone or with family?"

3

Popularity debate

In small groups, explain why one activity or TV genre is popular. Use simple reasons: fun, cheap, relaxing, interesting, social.
