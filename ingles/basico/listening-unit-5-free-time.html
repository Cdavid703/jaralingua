Friday After Class

Show / hide transcript

Nina: It is Friday after class. Mateo, what do you do in your free time?

Mateo: I like going out. I meet friends at the park, and sometimes I go to the movies. What about you?

Nina: I like staying in. I watch cooking shows twice a week, and I play video games on Saturday night.

Mateo: What kind of TV shows do you like?

Nina: I like cooking shows and documentaries. Do you watch TV every day?

Mateo: No, I do not. But I like documentaries too.

Nina: Let's meet on Sunday afternoon and watch a documentary at my house.

Mateo: Great idea.
