Workshop Brief

Students invent one regular day for a literary or superhero character and perform it as a four-scene comic strip.

02

Task Cycle

Activity Rounds

Follow the rounds in order. The teacher can stop after any round for quick pronunciation or grammar feedback.

  1. Choose a characterTeams choose a character such as Sherlock Holmes, Peter Pan, Little Red Riding Hood or a superhero.
  2. Plan four scenesTeams write morning, afternoon, evening and night routines with times.
  3. Draw or stage scenesStudents draw simple panels or freeze-frame each scene with body language.
  4. Perform the comicOne narrator reads, two actors perform, and one student asks the class comprehension questions.
  5. Peer feedbackClassmates identify the clearest routine sentence and one sentence to improve.
