Maya's Morning Routine

Show / hide transcript

Maya is a student and a receptionist. She wakes up at six o'clock every morning. She takes a shower, has breakfast, and listens to music on the bus. She starts work at eight. After work, she studies English at the institute. Maya usually goes to the gym on Tuesday and Thursday. She never runs in the morning because she is very busy. On Sundays, she sleeps late and visits her grandmother. Her routine is busy, but she likes it.
