BASIC COURSE 1 - FINAL WRITING TASK (20%)

" Indoor-outdoor activities"

TYPE OF TASK: e-mail

AUDIENCE: best friend

PURPOSE: describe indoor and outdoor activities

PROMPT: You have noticed that your best friend is feeling sad and tired. You want to help your friend feel happier and healthier, so you decide to write an e-mail suggesting some indoor and outdoor activities that can help your friend feel better, have more energy, and improve their health.

INSTRUCTIONS: In your email you must,
  1. Start your email with a friendly greeting.
  2. Suggest some indoor and outdoor activities to your friend.
  3. Give examples of activities that can help your friend feel better and stay healthy, for example yoga, walking, cycling, or cooking.
  4. Finish your email with a proper closing.
  5. Spend 50 minutes on this task.
  6. Write around 80 words.
