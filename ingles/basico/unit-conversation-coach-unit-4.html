How to answer better

Give the action, the time, and the frequency

Before recording, choose one useful structure. Then add a routine action, a clock time, a frequency word, a sport, or a healthy habit.

Goal: short routine sentences
TimeI wake up at...Use at for exact clock times.
FrequencyI usually...Put frequency before the main verb.
QuestionsHow often do you...?Use do before the subject.
Sportsplay / go / do / work outChoose the correct verb for the activity.

Attempt target

For each normal question, answer with one complete sentence. For the final healthy-day question, use three short sentences.

  • First attempt: use the sentence frames.
  • Second attempt: hide the help after question one.
  • Challenge: include time plus frequency in one answer.
Common mistake 1She wake up early.She wakes up early.
Common mistake 2I wake up in 6:30.I wake up at 6:30.
Common mistake 3I practice soccer.I play soccer.
Common mistake 4I make yoga.I do yoga.
