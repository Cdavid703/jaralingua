The Missing Notebook

Teacher: Good morning, class. It is Monday, and the English class is starting. Where is Karla?

Daniel: She is in the computer room. She is printing a document.

Ana: There is a blue backpack on the desk. There is also a laptop, two notebooks, and a red cellphone.

Ana: Is this your cellphone, Daniel?

Daniel: No, it is not. My cellphone is in my bag.

Karla: I am back. That is my cellphone, and those are my notebooks. Thank you.

Teacher: Great. Please open your book. Let's start the activity.
