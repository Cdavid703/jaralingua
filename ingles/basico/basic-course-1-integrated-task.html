Type of taskTravel magazine / lifestyle blog
AudienceReaders of a travel magazine or lifestyle blog
PurposeTo identify daily routines

Prompt: Listen to three people talking about their daily routines. First, answer the questions. Then write about your own daily routine.

Section 1 - 25 points

Listening

Listen to the protected audio a maximum of three times. Choose the best answer for each question.

0 / 3 listens

Section 2 - 25 points - 40 minutes

Integrated writing

You are writing a short text for a travel magazine or lifestyle blog. Describe your daily routine.

Include:
  • What time you get up.
  • What you do in the morning, afternoon, and evening.
  • How you go to work or school (if needed).
  • What you do in your free time.
  • Your opinion about your routine (is it busy? Relaxed? Interesting?).

Use simple sentences and organize your ideas in paragraphs.

Write at least 60 words.
