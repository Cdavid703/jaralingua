Healthy Wednesday

Read a short article about one student's routine, sports habits, healthy choices, and frequency words.

Practice Goal

Identify daily routines, times, sports verbs, frequency adverbs, and healthy habits in a short text.

01

Reading Instructions

Read the Routine

Look for time expressions, routine verbs, sports activities, and frequency words such as always, usually, sometimes, and never.

02

Reading Text

Healthy Wednesday

Every Wednesday, Jordan has a healthy routine. He gets up at six thirty and drinks a glass of water. He usually eats eggs, fruit, and bread for breakfast. At school, he walks during the break because he does not like sitting all morning. After class, Jordan plays basketball with two friends. He sometimes goes running in the park, but he never exercises late at night. At home, he does his homework before dinner. His mother cooks soup and rice. Jordan goes to bed at ten because he wants energy for Thursday.

03

Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

1. What time does Jordan get up?

2. What does Jordan drink in the morning?

3. What does he usually eat for breakfast?

4. What does Jordan do during the break?

5. What sport does Jordan play after class?

6. Where does he sometimes go running?

7. What does Jordan never do late at night?

8. Why does Jordan go to bed at ten?
