Practice Goal

Students move from controlled grammar to oral production: This is a notebook. → It is on the desk. → It is Ana's notebook. → Whose notebook is this?

Speaking model

02

Speaking sequence

From Word to Mini Report

The student should not jump directly into a long sentence. This ladder helps them build confidence.

1. Name itThis is a notebook. / These are keys.
2. Locate itIt is on the desk. / They are in the bag.
3. Say ownerIt is Ana's. / They are Carlos's.
4. AskWhose notebook is this?
1

Individual practice

Each student generates one model and reads it once.

2

Partner check

Partner asks one question: Where is it? or Whose object is it?

3

Oral report

Student reports without looking: This is a marker. It is Ana's marker. It is on the teacher's desk.

03

Question control

Ask About People, Things, Location, and Owner

This activity prepares the student for the roulette, the mystery object game, the listening questions, and the oral coach.

Thing

What is this? / What are these?

Location

Where is the notebook? / Where are the keys?

Person

Who is that? / Who is near the board?

Owner

Whose notebook is this? / Whose keys are these?

Quick rule

Use who for people and whose for possession. Do not say Who notebook is this? Say: Whose notebook is this?
