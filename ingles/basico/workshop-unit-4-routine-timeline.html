02

Task Cycle

Activity Rounds

Follow the rounds in order. The teacher can stop after any round for quick pronunciation or grammar feedback.

  1. Personal timelineStudents write six routine moments with times for one typical week.
  2. Partner interviewPartners ask What time do you...? How often do you...? and mark similar habits.
  3. Group synthesisGroups create a new timeline with activities they have in common.
  4. Routine reportGroups present: We usually study at night. We never run in the morning.
  5. Feedback clinicThe teacher highlights third-person -s only if students report about a classmate.
