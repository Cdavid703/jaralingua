Neighborhood Memory Challenge

Two teams reveal place cards, find matching pairs, listen to the vocabulary word, and pronounce only the place name before earning the point.

12 pairs24 cards6 x 4 boardVocabulary

How to play

  1. Team 1 chooses two hidden cards.
  2. If the places match, the student listens to the word.
  3. The student repeats only the vocabulary word clearly.
  4. If the cards do not match, the turn passes to the other team.
