01

Grammar Focus

Choose the correct structure

Do not translate directly from Spanish. In English, the verb changes depending on the activity. Read each structure carefully before you answer.

play + sport/gameTeam sports, ball sports, games.I play soccer. She plays basketball. They play chess.Do not say: practice soccer.
go + -ingMovement activities.I go running. We go swimming. He goes cycling.Do not say: go to running.
do + activityExercise, yoga, martial arts.I do yoga. She does karate. They do exercise.Do not say: make yoga.
work outGeneral exercise.I work out three times a week.Do not add: in after work out.
