02

Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

Choose the best answer. Some options are close, so use the whole conversation, not only one repeated word.

1. Which sentence best summarizes Ben's weekday morning routine?

2. Why is Ben's restaurant habit during the week important?

3. Which routine happens only sometimes, not always or usually?

4. What does Ben normally do after his workday ends?

5. Which statement about Ben's English study is accurate?

6. What contrast does Ben make between Saturday and Sunday?

7. What does Ben mean when he says he never wakes up early on Sundays?

8. Which pair of activities belongs to Anna's routine?

9. Which frequency word is used for an activity Ben does very little during the week?

10. Which idea is most strongly supported by the complete conversation?
