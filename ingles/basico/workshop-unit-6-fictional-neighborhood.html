01

Activity Logic

What students produce

Final product

Each team creates a short English description of an ideal or fictional neighborhood. The description must be detailed enough for an AI model to create a clear image. Then the team generates the image outside Jaralingua, uploads it here, and presents it orally.

  • Target length: 80-120 words.
  • Include at least six places in town.
  • Use there is / there are, location phrases, adjectives, and activities.
  • Explain one favorite place and one reason.

Why this replaces drawing

The important work is not artistic drawing. The important work is English precision. If the description is vague, the image will be weak. If the description is concrete, organized, and visual, the image will be useful for speaking practice.

02

Process

Four clear rounds

Plan.
Choose the name, style, places, and people in the neighborhood.
Describe.
Write an 80-120 word paragraph using Unit 6 language.
Prompt.
Transform the paragraph into a visual AI prompt with exact details.
Present.
Show the generated image and explain the neighborhood in English.
03

Rubric

What a strong submission needs

Description

80-120 words, clear sentences, and enough visual detail for the image.

Unit 6 language

Places in town, there is / there are, location phrases, adjectives, and activities.

Prompt quality

Specific places, clean style instructions, and no text/logos/watermarks in the image.

Speaking

The team explains the image naturally and answers short questions from classmates.

04

Model

Example description, prompt, and generated image

Example description in English

Welcome to Green Point. It is a quiet and green neighborhood for students. There is a big park in the center. There is a modern library next to the park. There are two small cafes across from the library. There is a supermarket near the bus stop, and there is a gym behind the supermarket. People walk in the park, read books in the library, and drink coffee in the cafes. My favorite place is the library because it is quiet and clean. The cafe opens at seven o'clock. I recommend Green Point because it is safe and friendly.

Example AI prompt

Create a clean professional 2D illustration of a fictional neighborhood called Green Point. It is a quiet, clean, safe, and friendly green neighborhood for students. Show a big park in the center, a modern library next to the park, two small cafes across from the library, a supermarket near a bus stop, and a gym behind the supermarket. Show people walking in the park, reading near the library, and drinking coffee at the cafes. Use a bright educational style, soft colors, clear streets, trees, sidewalks, and a friendly atmosphere. No text, no labels, no logos, no watermark.

Language Support

Useful sentence frames

There is a quiet park in the center. There are two cafes across from the library. The supermarket is next to the bus stop. People can walk, read, study, and relax. My favorite place is the library because it is clean. I recommend this neighborhood because it is safe.

Speaking Closure

Presentation model and questions

45-60 second model

This is Green Point. It is a quiet and green neighborhood. There is a big park in the center, and there are two cafes across from the library. The supermarket is near the bus stop. People can walk, read, drink coffee, and relax. My favorite place is the library because it is clean and quiet. I recommend this neighborhood because it is safe and friendly.

Team presentation

  • What is the name of your neighborhood?
  • What places are there?
  • Where is your favorite place?
  • What can people do there?
  • Why do you recommend it?

Class questions

  • Is there a quiet place?
  • Are there cafes or restaurants?
  • Where is the bus stop?
  • What time does the cafe open?
  • How can I get to the library?
