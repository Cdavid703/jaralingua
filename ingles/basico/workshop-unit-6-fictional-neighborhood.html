Practice Lab Workshop

Fictional Neighborhood Design Lab

Students design a fictional neighborhood with a clear map, describe places, routines, opening times, and write a short visitor blog entry recommending it.

01

Workshop Brief

Mission and Setup

This activity works better if it is treated as a small project, not only as a drawing task. Students must create a useful neighborhood, justify why people visit it, and speak from their own map.

Time

65-75 minutes

Grouping

Teams of 3 or 4

Main output

Map, oral tour, and short blog entry

Language focus

Places, there is / there are, time, adjectives, and simple recommendations

Required places
  • a park
  • a cafe or restaurant
  • a school or library
  • a supermarket or pharmacy
  • one free place chosen by the team
Required details
  • opening time for two places
  • one favorite place
  • one quiet place
  • one busy place
  • one weekend activity
Team roles
  • Map designer
  • Language checker
  • Time keeper
  • Presenter / blogger
02

Task Cycle

Activity Rounds

Follow the rounds in order. Each round produces evidence: a map decision, a sentence, or a speaking rehearsal. This avoids random drawings with little English production.

  1. Choose the neighborhood identity. Decide if it is quiet, modern, green, busy, safe, artistic, or student-friendly.
  2. Build the map. Place at least five places and draw streets clearly enough for another team to understand the area.
  3. Write place sentences. Produce at least three there is sentences and two there are sentences.
  4. Add time and routines. Decide when people go to two places and what they do there.
  5. Prepare the visitor blog. Write a short recommendation from the point of view of a visitor.
  6. Give the oral tour. One student presents the map; classmates ask two questions.

04

Functional Language

Language Bank and Final Product

There is a beautiful park near the cafe.There are two quiet streets in our neighborhood.The library opens at eight o'clock.People go to the gym after class.My favorite place is the cafe because it is friendly.You can visit the park on weekends.

Final product model

Welcome to Green Street. There is a big park and there are two cafes. The library opens at eight o'clock. People go to the park at five. My favorite place is the cafe because it is friendly and quiet. Visit Green Street on Saturday.

05

Oral Tour

Presentation and visitor questions

Each team presents for 90 seconds. The presentation must not be read word by word; students can use the map and worksheet as support.

Presenter 1

Names the neighborhood and describes the map using there is and there are.

Presenter 2

Explains times and routines: when people go, what people do, and where they go.

Visitors

Ask: What is your favorite place? What time does it open? Where can I eat? Is there a quiet place?

06

Teacher Check

Simple rubric

Map clarity

Places are visible and logically organized.

Grammar

Uses there is / there are correctly.

Unit vocabulary

Uses places, times, and adjectives from Unit 6.

Speaking

Explains the neighborhood clearly without only reading.
