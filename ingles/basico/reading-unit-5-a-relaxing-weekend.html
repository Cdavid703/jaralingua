Practice Lab - Unit 5 Reading

A Relaxing Weekend

Read a short school blog review about staying in, going out, free-time activities, TV, and preferences.

Unit 5ReadingFree time8 questions
Start readingBack to Unit 5 folder
Practice Goal

Identify indoor and outdoor free-time activities, preferences, frequency, reasons with because, and TV habits.

01

Reading Instructions

Read the Review

Look for activities people do at home and outside. Notice like, prefer, because, sometimes, and usually.

02

Reading Text

A Relaxing Weekend

For the school blog, Nina writes a short review of her weekend. On Saturday morning, she stays in and listens to music. She usually watches a comedy show because it is funny. In the afternoon, she reads a book and chats with her cousin online. On Sunday, Nina goes out with two friends. They go to the park, take photos, and eat pizza near the cinema. Nina likes going out, but she prefers quiet activities at home when she is tired. She says free time is important because it helps students rest and feel happy.

03

Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

1. What does Nina write?

2. What does Nina do on Saturday morning?

3. Why does she watch a comedy show?

4. Who does Nina chat with online?

5. Who goes out with Nina on Sunday?

6. Where do Nina and her friends eat pizza?

7. What does Nina prefer when she is tired?

8. Why is free time important?
