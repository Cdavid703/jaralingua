04

Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

Choose the best answer. These questions ask you to understand the route, the purpose of the landmarks, and the relationship between places.

1. What is the main purpose of the conversation?

2. Which complete route matches Daniel's instructions?

3. Why is the bank important in Daniel's directions?

4. If Emma reaches the bank, what should she do next?

5. Which sentence best explains the relationship between the library and the school?

6. Emma asks about the cafe because...

7. Which answer uses across from correctly according to the video?

8. What can we understand about the bus stop?

9. Which instruction would be wrong for Emma?

10. Why does Emma repeat the directions at the end?

Short answer

In your own words, how does Emma get to the library? Write 2 or 3 short sentences.

Model answer: She goes straight ahead for two blocks. She turns left at the bank. Then she walks one block. The library is next to the school.
05

After You Watch

Mini Speaking Practice

Use the same model with a partner. One student asks for a place; the other gives simple directions.

Student A

Ask for a place: Excuse me. Where is the library? You can change the place: bank, cafe, supermarket, school, or bus stop.

Student B

Give directions: Go straight ahead for two blocks. Turn left at the bank. The library is next to the school.

Variation 1

Student A asks: Where is the cafe? Student B answers using across from and library.

Variation 2

Student A asks: Where is the bus stop? Student B answers using in front of and supermarket.
