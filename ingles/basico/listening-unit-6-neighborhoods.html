A Walk in My Neighborhood

Play the audio and focus on the details.

Show / hide transcript

My neighborhood is small, but it is very nice. There is a beautiful park near my apartment, and there are two cafes across from the park. My favorite cafe opens at seven thirty in the morning. On Saturdays, I have breakfast there with my sister. There is also a library next to the supermarket. It is quiet and modern. At five o'clock, many people walk in the park. I think it is the best place in my city because it is clean, friendly, and safe.
