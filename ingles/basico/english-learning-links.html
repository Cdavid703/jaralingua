How to Use Them

Simple Study Routine

  1. Check meaning firstUse WordReference or Cambridge Dictionary before translating a full sentence.
  2. Listen in contextUse YouGlish, ELLLO, VOA or BBC Learning English to hear real examples.
  3. Practice activelyUse LyricsTraining, Perfect English Grammar or Agendaweb to complete exercises, not only read.
  4. Bring one example to classWrite one new word, one useful sentence and one question for the teacher.
