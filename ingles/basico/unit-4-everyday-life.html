Unit 4: Everyday Life

Learning Outcome

By the end of this unit, you will be able to describe typical mornings, daily routines, sports routines, and healthy life activities.

01

Routines

A Typical Morning

Use simple present verbs to describe what you do every day.

Morning Verbs

I wake up at 6:00.Use at for clock time.
I listen to music.Use base verbs after I/you/we/they.

Questions

Do you practice sports?Yes, I do. / No, I do not.
What do you do in the morning?I take a shower and have breakfast.
02

Frequency

Sports and Work Routines

Add frequency words to explain how often activities happen.

How Often?

How often do you go to the gym?I go twice a week.
What sport do you practice?I practice soccer.

Habits

I usually study at night.Usually means most days.
I never run in the morning.Never means zero times.
Teacher's Tip

Frequency words usually go before the main verb: I usually study.
