Eyewitness Testimony

Practice Goal

Students ask and answer yes/no questions and give short physical descriptions of suspects using complete ideas.

01

Case File

What Happened?

Recently, there was a burglary next to your house. When the incident took place, you were arriving home and you saw four suspicious people leaving your neighborhood in a hurry. The police officers have contacted you because they need your description of the suspects.

02

Before Playing

Example Conversation

Open example conversation
Police Officer

Hi! Thanks for helping us.

Eyewitness

Hi! No problem.

Police Officer

Is the suspect wearing black clothes?

Eyewitness

Yes, he is wearing a black jacket.

Police Officer

Does he have glasses?

Eyewitness

Yes, he has glasses.

Police Officer

Does he have a hat or a cap?

Eyewitness

Yes, he has a black cap.

Police Officer

Can you describe him?

Eyewitness

He is tall and slim. He is wearing a black cap, glasses, gloves, and a red backpack.

Police Officer

Great. Thank you. See you!

Eyewitness

You're welcome. Bye!
