Practice Goal

By the end of this activity, you will be able to understand a short first-day-of-class conversation and identify key information about names, age, greetings, and goodbyes.

Show / hide transcript

Alex: Hello! Good morning.

Sarah: Hi! Good morning. Are you a new student?

Alex: Yes, I am. My name is Alex. What is your name?

Sarah: My name is Sarah. It is nice to meet you, Alex.

Alex: Nice to meet you too, Sarah. I am twenty-two years old. How old are you?

Sarah: I am twenty-one years old. Well, class is starting soon. Have a great day!

Alex: Thank you! Goodbye, Sarah.

Sarah: Bye!

03

Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

1. What is the first greeting used in the conversation?

2. Is Alex a new student?

3. How old is Alex?

4. How old is Sarah?

5. What are they doing at the end of the conversation?
