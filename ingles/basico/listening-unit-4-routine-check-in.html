Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

Listen to the conversation and choose the best answer. This follows the same multiple-choice format used in the other listening activities.

1. What is the main topic of the conversation?

2. What time does Daniel usually wake up on weekdays?

3. What does Daniel do after he wakes up?

4. Why does Daniel hardly ever skip breakfast?

5. What does Mia say about exercise in the morning?

6. What does Mia mean when she says, "I am a night owl"?

7. How often does Mia work out?

8. Which activities does Daniel do?

9. What does Daniel want to cut down on?

10. What does the teacher ask students to write at the end?
