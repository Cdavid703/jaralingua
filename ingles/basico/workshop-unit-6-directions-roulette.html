Practice Lab - Unit 6

Directions Map Roulette

The teacher chooses a map, the roulette chooses a student, and the second roulette chooses a destination. The student gives clear spoken directions from the starting boy to the selected place.

Speaking GameDirectionsPlaces in TownMap Reading
Start activity Back to Unit 6
Practice Goal

Students will give step-by-step directions using go straight, turn left, turn right, cross the street, go past, on your left, on your right, next to, and across from.

01

Teacher Setup

How to run the activity

First, load the student names. Then choose Map 1 or Map 2. Spin the student roulette and then spin the destination roulette. The selected student looks at the active map and explains how to get from the boy to the destination.

1

Choose the map

The teacher controls which image the class uses.

2

Spin the student

The roulette selects the participant for the round.

3

Spin the destination

The second roulette selects where the student must go.

4

Give directions

The student gives complete spoken directions and classmates verify the route.

02

Map Selection

Teacher chooses the active map

Map 1 - Neighborhood route

Start at the boy near the bus stop. Some places are close, but several destinations require walking across the map with turns and crossings.

Visible places in this map

Use the icons as references. The image has no labels on purpose, so the teacher can control the vocabulary orally.

03

Language Support

Useful expressions before spinning

Go straight. Turn left. Turn right. Cross the street. Go past the park. Walk two blocks. It is on your left. It is on your right. It is next to the cafe. It is across from the library.
Model answer

Go straight for two blocks. Turn right. Cross the street. Go past the park. The library is on your left.

06

Teacher Rubric

Fast oral feedback

Route clarity

Can classmates follow the route without confusion?

Direction verbs

Does the student use go straight, turn left/right, cross, and go past?

Location phrases

Does the student finish with on your left/right, next to, or across from?

Pronunciation

Are places and direction words clear enough for A1 communication?
