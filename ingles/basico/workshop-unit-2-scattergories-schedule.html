01

Game Brief

Mission and Setup

The roulette selects a student from the class list. The teacher chooses one of the ten cards, asks the selected student to describe the image, and then clicks the card to reveal the model sentence.

Time

35-45 minutes

Grouping

Whole class or teams

Main output

Complete sentences with be + place

Target language

Where is...? He / She is...

  • Paste student names
  • Spin to select one student
  • Choose a picture card manually
  • Ask: Where is he? / Where is she?
  • Click the card to reveal the model answer
