How to answer better

Build complete answers, not isolated words

Before recording, choose one useful structure. Then add a real person, a family relationship, one adjective, one physical feature, or a simple reason.

Goal: complete short sentences
Name and relationshipHis / Her name is...Use this to introduce the person clearly.
PersonalityHe / She is...Use be before adjectives: kind, funny, smart.
AppearanceHe / She has...Use has for hair, eyes, glasses, and features.
Reasonbecause he / she is...Use a short reason to make the answer stronger.

Final model answer
My favorite person is my sister. Her name is Sofia. She is kind and funny. She has long hair. She is important to me because she helps me.

Attempt target

For each normal question, answer with at least one complete sentence. For the final description, use three short sentences.

  • First attempt: use the sentence frames.
  • Second attempt: hide the help after question one.
  • Challenge: answer without reading the model.
