Learning Outcome

By the end of this unit, you will be able to describe famous people, classmates, and family members using simple be-verb sentences and yes/no questions.

01

Descriptions

National and Foreign Celebrities

Use simple vocabulary to say who people are and what they do.

Identity

He is an actor.Use he for a man or boy.
She is a famous singer.Use she for a woman or girl.

Groups

They are soccer players.Use they for two or more people.
They are from Colombia.Use from for origin.
02

Questions

People We Know

Ask and answer yes/no questions with the verb to be.

Questions

Are you busy?Yes, I am. / No, I am not.
Is he your classmate?Yes, he is. / No, he is not.

Negative Sentences

I am not late.Use not after am.
It is not hard work.Contraction: it isn't hard work.
Teacher's Tip

In short answers, do not repeat everything every time: Yes, she is is enough.

02 Pronouns and Possessives

When you describe favorite people in your family, choose the correct subject pronoun and possessive word.

SubjectPossessiveUseExample
HeHisOne male personHe is my brother. His name is Daniel.
SheHerOne female personShe is my aunt. Her name is Laura.
TheyTheirTwo or more peopleThey are my parents. Their names are Ana and Luis.
ItItsA pet or thingIt is my dog. Its name is Toby.
03 Simple Family Structures

Introduce One Person

This is my sister.Say the relationship.
Her name is Sofia.Use her for a female person.
She is nineteen.Use is for one person.

Introduce Two People

These are my grandparents.Use these are for two or more people.
Their names are Grace and Paul.Use their for two names.
They are happy.Use are with they.
