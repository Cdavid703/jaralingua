Know the task

What happens in the real Final Oral Task?

You have an informal conversation with a visitor who is new to your neighborhood. Speak naturally. Use short notes, but do not read a complete prepared text.

01

Describe places

Talk about a supermarket, park, school, restaurant, or another place near your home.

02

Locate them

Use near, next to, across from, or on the corner.

03

Interact

Answer the visitor and ask your own questions. This must feel like a conversation.

04

Keep it natural

The complete conversation should last approximately two to three minutes.
