# Unit 4 Listening: Routine Check-in

## Routine Check-in
File: `unit-4-routine-check-in-listening.mp3`

Teacher: Today, ask about daily routines and healthy habits.
Mia: Daniel, what time do you wake up on weekdays?
Daniel: I usually wake up at six thirty. I get up quickly, take a shower, and have breakfast at seven.
Mia: Do you always eat breakfast?
Daniel: Almost always. I hardly ever skip breakfast because I feel tired without food. What about you?
Mia: I wake up at seven, but I never exercise in the morning. I am a night owl.
Daniel: Really? When do you work out?
Mia: I work out three times a week, on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. I usually do yoga at night.
Daniel: I play soccer on Saturdays at four. I also go running once a week.
Mia: That's healthy. Do you drink soda?
Daniel: Sometimes, but I want to cut down on soda and drink more water.
Teacher: Good. Now write one true sentence and one question about your partner's routine.
