# Mock Integrated Task - Daily Routines

File: `basic-integrated-task-mock-us.mp3`

## Script

Narrator: Listen to Daniel, Emma, and Noah talk about their daily routines.

Daniel: On weekdays, I get up at six fifteen. I take a shower, and then I have eggs and toast for breakfast. I ride my bicycle to the restaurant where I work. I start at seven thirty and finish at three. After work, I usually play soccer with my friends. In the evening, I watch television and go to bed at ten thirty.

Emma: I get up at seven o'clock. I have cereal and coffee, and I take the bus to language school. I like to read a book on the bus. My classes are from nine to one. In the afternoon, I study at the library. After dinner, I call my sister and tell her about my day.

Noah: I work from home, so I get up at eight thirty. I do not eat breakfast, but I drink orange juice. I start work on my computer at nine. I have a sandwich for lunch at twelve thirty. After lunch, I walk my dog. In the evening, I cook dinner and play video games.
