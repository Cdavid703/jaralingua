# BASIC COURSE 1 – INTEGRATED TASK (20%) - Daily Routines

File: `basic-integrated-task-real.local.mp3`

## Script

Narrator: Listen to Olivia, Marcus, and Sofia talk about their daily routines.

Olivia: I get up at five forty-five every weekday. First, I feed my cat. Then I have oatmeal and tea for breakfast. I take the subway to the bakery at six forty and start work at seven thirty. I finish at three in the afternoon. After work, I buy food at the supermarket. I usually go to bed at nine thirty.

Marcus: My alarm rings at seven fifteen. I have pancakes and milk before I drive to college. My first class is chemistry at nine o'clock. I eat lunch in the college cafeteria. In the afternoon, I play basketball with my classmates. In the evening, I do my homework and listen to music before bed.

Sofia: I get up at ten because I start work at noon. I do yoga in my living room and have eggs for breakfast. I take the bus to the community center, where I work until six. After dinner, I call my parents. Before I go to bed, I read a book for about twenty minutes.
