# Basic Course 1 - Final Oral Task Mock

## Production specification

- Topic: **My Neighborhood**.
- Character: **Emma**, a friendly visitor who is new to the student's neighborhood.
- Provider: ElevenLabs Text to Speech.
- Voice direction: professional adult woman, neutral American English, warm, reassuring, and natural.
- Student level: Basic Course 1 / CEFR A1.
- Delivery: clear articulation, short natural pauses, and supportive intonation without sounding childish.
- Format: MP3, 44.1 kHz, 128 kbps.
- Playback controls in the page: **0.75x** and **1x only**. The default speed is 1x.
- Accessibility: every clip must have the exact transcript available to the interface, although it may remain visually hidden during the realistic mock.
- Important: the clips never say a score and never tell the student that work will be sent to the teacher.

The generator reads only the spoken text placed after each `File:` line and before the next level-two heading.
