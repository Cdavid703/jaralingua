# Unit 6 Neighborhoods - Lesson Audio Scripts

## Unit 6 Introduction

File: `unit-6-introduction.mp3`

In this unit, you are going to talk about neighborhoods and places in the city. You will learn words for common places, such as park, library, supermarket, café, bank, hospital, and bus stop. You will also learn how to use there is and there are, how to ask for directions, how to give simple directions, how to say times, and how to recommend your favorite place in the city.

## Directions Conversation

File: `unit-6-directions-conversation.mp3`

Ana: Excuse me, where is the library?

Daniel: It is near the park. Go straight for two blocks and turn right at the café.

Ana: Is it next to the supermarket?

Daniel: Yes, it is. It is across from the park.

Ana: What time does it open?

Daniel: It opens at eight o'clock.

Ana: Great. Is there a bus stop near the library?

Daniel: Yes, there is. It is just around the corner.

Ana: Thank you.

Daniel: You're welcome.

## City Expressions

File: `unit-6-city-expressions.mp3`

Listen and repeat. How can I get to the library? Go straight for two blocks. Turn left at the bank. Turn right at the café. Go past the supermarket. Walk down Main Street. Stop by the café after class. Check out the new restaurant downtown. Let's meet up at the bus stop.

## Unit 6 Idioms

File: `unit-6-idioms.mp3`

Just around the corner means very near. The café is just around the corner. Hit the road means to leave or start a trip. It is late. Let's hit the road. In the middle of nowhere means very far from important places. The hotel is in the middle of nowhere. A stone's throw away means very near. The library is a stone's throw away from the park.

## Final Speaking Model

File: `unit-6-final-speaking-model.mp3`

My favorite place in my city is the library. There is a park across from it, and there are two cafés near it. To get there, go straight and turn right at the supermarket. The library is on your left. It opens at eight o'clock. I go there at four p.m. after class. It is quiet, clean, and modern. The café is just around the corner, so I sometimes stop by after studying.
