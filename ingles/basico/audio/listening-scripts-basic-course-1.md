## Unit 1 - First Day at the Business Event
File: `unit-1-all-about-you-listening.mp3`

Sara: Good morning. Welcome to the registration table. My name is Sara.
Alex: Good morning. My name is Alex Rivera. I am twenty-two years old, and I live in Medellin. Today is my first day at this business event.
Sara: Nice to meet you, Alex. Could you please spell your last name?
Alex: Yes. R-I-V-E-R-A.
Sara: Thank you, Alex. I am twenty-one years old, and I live in Envigado.
Alex: Nice to meet you, Sara.
Sara: Nice to meet you too, Alex. Have a great day.
Alex: Thank you. Goodbye.

## Unit 2 - The Missing Notebook
File: `unit-2-in-class-listening.mp3`

Teacher: Good morning, class. It is Monday, and the English class is starting. Where is Karla?
Daniel: She is in the computer room. She is printing a document.
Ana: There is a blue backpack on the desk. There is also a laptop, two notebooks, and a red cellphone.
Ana: Is this your cellphone, Daniel?
Daniel: No, it is not. My cellphone is in my bag.
Karla: I am back. That is my cellphone, and those are my notebooks. Thank you.
Teacher: Great. Please open your book. Let's start the activity.

## Unit 3 - My Favorite Singer
File: `unit-3-favorite-people-listening.mp3`

Luis: This is my favorite singer. Her name is Emma Lee. She is from Canada, and she is twenty-five years old.
Partner: Is she an actor?
Luis: No, she is not. She is a singer. She is creative and friendly.
Partner: Is she famous?
Luis: Yes, she is. And this is my family photo.
Partner: Who is the woman in the photo?
Luis: She is my mother. This is my brother. He is sixteen, and he is a student.
Partner: Nice photo, Luis.

## Unit 4 - Maya's Morning Routine
File: `unit-4-everyday-life-listening.mp3`

Maya is a student and a receptionist. She wakes up at six o'clock every morning. She takes a shower, has breakfast, and listens to music on the bus. She starts work at eight. After work, she studies English at the institute. Maya usually goes to the gym on Tuesday and Thursday. She never runs in the morning because she is very busy. On Sundays, she sleeps late and visits her grandmother. Her routine is busy, but she likes it.

## Unit 5 - Friday After Class
File: `unit-5-free-time-listening.mp3`

Nina: It is Friday after class. Mateo, what do you do in your free time?
Mateo: I like going out. I meet friends at the park, and sometimes I go to the movies. What about you?
Nina: I like staying in. I watch cooking shows twice a week, and I play video games on Saturday night.
Mateo: What kind of TV shows do you like?
Nina: I like cooking shows and documentaries. Do you watch TV every day?
Mateo: No, I do not. But I like documentaries too.
Nina: Let's meet on Sunday afternoon and watch a documentary at my house.
Mateo: Great idea.

## Unit 6 - A Walk in My Neighborhood
File: `unit-6-neighborhoods-listening.mp3`

My neighborhood is small, but it is very nice. There is a beautiful park near my apartment, and there are two cafes across from the park. My favorite cafe opens at seven thirty in the morning. On Saturdays, I have breakfast there with my sister. There is also a library next to the supermarket. It is quiet and modern. At five o'clock, many people walk in the park. I think it is the best place in my city because it is clean, friendly, and safe.
