## The Blue Backpack
File: `unit-2-the-blue-backpack-listening.mp3`

Narrator: Good morning. Leo is new in English class. He sits next to Ana and opens his black backpack.
Leo: Excuse me, Ana. There is a blue backpack under my desk. Is this your backpack?
Ana: No, it isn't. My backpack is on the chair. Maybe it is Sofia's.
Teacher: Good question, Leo. Class, whose backpack is this?
Daniel: I think it is Sofia's backpack. Her notebook is inside, and her red marker is in the front pocket.
Sofia: Yes, it is mine. Thank you. I am at the door because I am late today.
Teacher: That's okay, Sofia. Please sit down. Leo, can you write your name and phone number on this class card?
Leo: Yes. My name is Leo Rivera. I am fourteen, and my phone number is 555-0182.
Narrator: The teacher points to the board. The students repeat: this, that, mine, yours, and Sofia's.
Narrator: The class starts, and everyone opens Unit Two.
