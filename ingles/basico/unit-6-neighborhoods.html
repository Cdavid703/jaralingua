Learning Outcome

By the end of this unit, you will be able to describe places in a neighborhood, tell the time, and talk about favorite places in the city.

01

Places

Nice Places in a Neighborhood

Use there is and there are to describe what exists in a place.

Singular

There is a cafe.Use there is for one place.
There is a library near my house.Use near for close places.

Plural

There are two parks.Use there are for more than one.
There are many restaurants.Many means a lot.
02

Time and Adjectives

Favorite Places in the City

Use time expressions and adjectives to explain why places are important.

Time

What time is it?It is seven thirty.
Let's have dinner at 8:00.Use let's for suggestions.

Adjectives

The best cafeUse best for your top choice.
The biggest parkUse biggest to compare size.
Teacher's Tip

Start simple: There is a park. It is beautiful. I go there at 5:00.
