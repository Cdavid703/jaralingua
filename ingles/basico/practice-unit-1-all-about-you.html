02

Conversation Game

Business Event Mixer

Use the introduction you generated, then move around the room and collect real information from classmates.

1

Meet three people

Greet each classmate, introduce yourself, ask their name, age, city, and spelling, then say goodbye politely.

2

Find two similarities

Write two things you have in common: same city, similar age, same neighborhood, same job, or same interest.

3

Report to the class

Tell the group: "This is Ana. She is twenty-one. She lives in Bello. We both live near Medellin."

Teacher interaction

The teacher becomes the event host and asks follow-up questions: "Could you spell that?", "Where do you live?", and "Who has something in common with you?"
