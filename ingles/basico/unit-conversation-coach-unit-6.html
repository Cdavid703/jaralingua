How to answer better

Name the place, give the location, and add one detail

Before recording, choose one useful structure. Then add a place, a location word, a direction, an opening time, or a reason for recommending it.

Goal: clear neighborhood answers
ExistenceThere is / There are...Use there is for one place and there are for two or more.
LocationIt is next to...Use next to, across from, near, or on your left.
DirectionsGo straight and turn...Give short steps from the starting point.
TimeIt opens at...Use at for opening and meeting times.

Attempt target

For each normal question, answer with one complete sentence. For the final neighborhood guide question, use three short sentences.

Common mistake 1There is two cafes.There are two cafes.
Common mistake 2The bank is in your left.The bank is on your left.
Common mistake 3Turn right in the cafe.Turn right at the cafe.
Common mistake 4The library open at eight.The library opens at eight.
