Practice Lab - Unit 3 Reading

A Family Photo

Read a short description of a family photo and answer questions about people, relationships, descriptions, and likes.

Unit 3ReadingFamily and descriptions8 questions
Start readingBack to Unit 3 folder
Practice Goal

Understand a simple family description and identify relationships, ages, physical descriptions, personality, and likes.

01

Reading Instructions

Find the People in the Photo

Read the description and pay attention to family words, he/she, possessives, adjectives, and likes.

02

Reading Text

A Family Photo

Camila shows a family photo on her phone. In the photo, her father, Luis, is tall and quiet. He likes old movies. Her mother, Rosa, is short and friendly. She likes cooking and music. Camila's brother, Diego, is sixteen. He has short black hair and brown eyes. He likes soccer, but he does not like homework. Camila's sister, Ana, is ten. She is funny and very active. The family dog is in the photo too. Its name is Rocky, and it is small. Camila says, "My family is different, but we are very close."

03

Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

1. What does Camila show?

2. Who is tall and quiet?

3. What does Rosa like?

4. How old is Diego?

5. What does Diego look like?

6. What does Diego not like?

7. What is Ana like?

8. Which sentence is true about Rocky?
