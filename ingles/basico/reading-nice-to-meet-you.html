Practice Lab - Unit 1 Reading

A New Student Card

Read a short A1 story about a new student, personal information, spelling, greetings, and saying goodbye.

Unit 1ReadingPersonal information8 questions
Start readingBack to Unit 1 folder
Practice Goal

Understand a short beginner reading and identify names, age, contact information, spelling, and polite expressions.

01

Reading Instructions

Read for Key Information

Read slowly. Underline the personal information in the story: name, age, email, phone number, last name, favorite color, and class day.

02

Reading Text

A New Student Card

Today is Lina's first day in an English class. She sits next to Mateo and opens her student card. The card has her name, age, email, and phone number. Lina is twenty years old. Her email is simple, but Mateo asks her to spell it slowly. The teacher says, "Please write your last name and your favorite color." Lina writes "Garcia" and "blue." She feels nervous at first, but the class is friendly. At the end of class, Mateo says, "Nice to meet you." Lina smiles and says, "Nice to meet you too. See you on Monday."

03

Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

1. Who is new in the English class?

2. Who sits next to Lina?

3. What information is on the student card?

4. How old is Lina?

5. What does Mateo ask Lina to spell?

6. What is Lina's favorite color?

7. How does Lina feel at first?

8. When will Lina see Mateo again?
