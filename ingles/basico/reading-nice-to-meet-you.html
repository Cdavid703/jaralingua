Reading Practice: Nice to Meet You

Read a short conversation between two students and answer comprehension questions about names, age, greetings, and polite expressions.

Reading Practice Comprehension Quiz Beginner Level
Start reading Back to Practice Lab
Practice Goal

By the end of this activity, you will be able to understand a short introductory conversation and identify key information from the text.

01

Reading Instructions

Read the Conversation

Read the conversation carefully. Pay attention to the greeting, the students’ names, their ages, and how they say goodbye.

Step 1

Read slowly Focus on the meaning of each sentence.

Step 2

Answer the questions Use the information from the conversation.
02

Reading Text

Nice to Meet You

Emma

Hello! Good afternoon.

Liam

Hi! Good afternoon.

Emma

My name is Emma. What is your name?

Liam

My name is Liam. It is nice to meet you, Emma.

Emma

Nice to meet you too. I am 19 years old. How old are you?

Liam

I am 21 years old.

Emma

Great! Goodbye, Liam!

Liam

Bye!

03

Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

1. What is the boy's name?

2. How old is Emma?

3. What greeting do they use?
