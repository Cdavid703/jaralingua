Vocabulary and Grammar

Cardinal and Ordinal Numbers

Learn the difference between counting numbers and order numbers, how to build them, and the main spelling exceptions.

Learning Outcome

By the end of this page, students can use cardinal numbers for quantity and ordinal numbers for position, dates, order, floors, and rankings.

01

Core Difference

How Many vs. Which Position

Cardinal numbers

Use cardinal numbers to count quantity.

I have two notebooks.Quantity: two.
There are ten students.Quantity: ten.

Ordinal numbers

Use ordinal numbers to show order or position.

She is first.Position: first place.
My birthday is on the fifth.Date: the fifth day.
02

Construction

How To Build Ordinal Numbers

Most ordinal numbers add -th to the cardinal number. However, the first numbers and some spellings change.

CardinalPronunciationOrdinalPronunciationNote
one/wun/first/furst/Irregular.
two/too/second/SEK-und/Irregular.
three/three/third/third/Irregular spelling.
four/for/fourth/forth/Add -th.
five/fiv/fifth/fifth/ve changes to f.
eight/ayt/eighth/ayth/Only one t.
nine/nain/ninth/nainth/Drop the e.
twelve/twelv/twelfth/twelfth/ve changes to f.
twenty/TWEN-tee/twentieth/TWEN-tee-eth/y changes to ie + th.
03

Compound Numbers

21st, 32nd, 43rd, 54th

In compound numbers, only the last number decides the ordinal ending.

twenty-one -> twenty-first thirty-two -> thirty-second forty-three -> forty-third fifty-four -> fifty-fourth one hundred and one -> one hundred and first two hundred and twenty-five -> two hundred and twenty-fifth
