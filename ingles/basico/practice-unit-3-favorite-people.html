Guess Who Game

Play a real classroom guessing game with professions, appearance clues, yes/no questions, and one student selected by roulette.

Practice Goal

Students ask yes/no questions in English to identify a hidden character from the professions board.

01

Student Instructions

How to Play Guess Who?

The teacher loads the student list and spins the student roulette. The selected student turns around and does not look at the TV screen. The student may use a phone to check the available character list. Then, the teacher spins the character roulette and the secret character appears on the screen for the class.

1

Spin for a student

The roulette chooses one student. That student turns away from the TV.

2

Spin for a character

The teacher selects the secret character with the character roulette. The class can see the result.

3

Ask yes/no questions

The student asks questions in English. The teacher and classmates answer only yes or no.

4

Find the character

When the student knows the answer, the student asks: “Is the character the ...?”

When the teacher clicks Character found, the student and the character are removed from the active lists. Then the next round can start.
