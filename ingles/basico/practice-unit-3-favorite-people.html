Survey Studio

Favorite People Interview

Students interview classmates about famous people, classmates, and family members, then present short descriptions.

1

Celebrity survey

Ask five classmates: "Who is your favorite singer?", "Is she famous?", "Is he Colombian?", "Are they soccer players?"

2

Photo description

Show or imagine a photo of a family member or celebrity. Describe the person using be-verb sentences and adjectives.

3

Guess who

Give three clues without saying the name. Classmates ask yes/no questions until they guess the person.

Mini presentation

Prepare a 45-second presentation: "This is my favorite person. He is... He is from... He is famous because..." The class asks two yes/no questions.
