My Favorite Singer

Play the audio and focus on the details.

Show / hide transcript

Luis: This is my favorite singer. Her name is Emma Lee. She is from Canada, and she is twenty-five years old.

Partner: Is she an actor?

Luis: No, she is not. She is a singer. She is creative and friendly.

Partner: Is she famous?

Luis: Yes, she is. And this is my family photo.

Partner: Who is the woman in the photo?

Luis: She is my mother. This is my brother. He is sixteen, and he is a student.

Partner: Nice photo, Luis.

Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

1. Who shows a photo?

2. Who is Emma Lee?

3. Where is Emma from?

4. How old is Emma?

5. How does Luis describe Emma?

6. Is Emma an actor?

7. What second photo does Luis show?

8. Who is in Luis's family photo?

9. How old is Luis's brother?

10. What does the partner say at the end?
