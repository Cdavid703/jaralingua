Comprehension Check

Answer the Questions

Choose the best answer. The options use similar vocabulary, so listen for who does each activity, how often, and why.

1. What does Daniel usually do with his friends?

2. Which two quiet activities does Sofia like?

3. What does Sofia always do after class?

4. Why does Sofia prefer staying in?

5. What does Sofia sometimes do on weekends?

6. Which sentence is true about Daniel's Saturdays?

7. What does Daniel's brother prefer doing?

8. When does Sofia's brother always watch TV?

9. What kind of TV shows does Sofia's brother like?

10. What technology habit does Sofia describe?

11. What does "uses technology too much" mean here?

12. How are Daniel and Sofia's preferences different?

13. What do Daniel and Sofia's brother have in common?

14. What is the main contrast at the end?

15. What is the best summary of the conversation?
