Routine Lab

Timeline and Character Day

Students compare real routines and then invent a daily routine for a character.

1

Weekly timeline

Write six moments from your week with times: wake up, study, work, exercise, relax, sleep. Ask a partner about each moment.

2

Common habits board

Find three activities you both do. Report: "We usually study at night. We never run in the morning."

3

Character routine

Choose Sherlock Holmes, Peter Pan, Little Red Riding Hood, or a local hero. Describe one regular day in that character's life.

Comic strip speaking

Create four quick scenes of the character's day. One student narrates, one acts, one asks "How often...?", and one gives feedback.
