The Blue Backpack, Part Two

This reading is the second part of the listening story The Blue Backpack. First, read the text silently. Then read it with a partner and answer the questions. Use complete answers when your teacher asks you to explain.

02

Reading Text

The Blue Backpack: Part Two

After class, Sofia opens her blue backpack. Her notebook is there, but her red marker is not in the front pocket. Leo sees a marker on Ana's desk. He asks, "Is this Sofia's marker?" Ana answers, "No, this marker is mine. Sofia's marker is on the teacher's table." Daniel points to the table. There are two markers: one red and one green. Sofia says, "The red marker is mine, and the green marker is the teacher's." The teacher smiles and says, "Good work. You can use English to solve small classroom problems." Then Leo writes his name, age, and phone number on a class card. Sofia helps him spell his last name.

03

Comprehension Check

Develop the Questions

1. What does Sofia open after class?

2. What object is missing from the front pocket?

3. Where does Leo see a marker first?

4. What does Ana say about the marker on her desk?

5. Where is Sofia's marker?

6. Which marker is the teacher's?

7. What can the students use English to solve?

8. What personal information does Leo write?
