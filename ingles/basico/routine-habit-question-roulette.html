02

Quick Review

Frequency adverbs and sentence structure

Use frequency adverbs to say how often something happens. In basic simple present sentences, the frequency adverb usually goes before the main verb.

always100% usually80-90% often60-70% sometimes40-50% rarely10-20% hardly everalmost never never0%
AffirmativeSubject + frequency adverb + verb + complement.I usually wake up at 6:00.She often drinks water.
QuestionHow often do you + verb + complement?How often do you work out?How often do you study English?
Short answerSubject + frequency adverb + verb + extra detail.I sometimes go running after class.I never drink soda at night.
Third personHe/She + frequency adverb + verb-s + complement.She usually plays soccer.He often goes cycling.
03

Activity Structures

Sports, exercise, and healthy habits

Use the correct verb with each activity. These structures help you answer many questions from the roulette.

play + sport/gameUse it for team sports, ball sports, and games.I play soccer on Saturdays.Not: I practice soccer.
go + -ingUse it for movement activities.We go swimming twice a week.Not: We go to swimming.
do + activityUse it for exercise, yoga, and martial arts.She does yoga in the evening.Not: She makes yoga.
work outUse it for general exercise.I work out three times a week.Not: I work out in the gym three times.
