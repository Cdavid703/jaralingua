Learning Outcome

By the end of this unit, you will be able to name classroom places and objects, ask where people or things are, and talk about simple classroom actions.

01

Places and Activities

Recognizing Locations

Use classroom and campus vocabulary to say where people are and what they are doing.

Places

in classThey are in class.
in the libraryShe is in the library.
in the computer roomHe is in the computer room.

Actions

studyingShe is studying.
workingHe is at work.
readingThey are reading.
Teacher's Tip

Use in for rooms and places: in class, in the library, in the bathroom.

02

Questions

Asking About Location

Use where questions to find people and objects in the classroom.

People

Where is Karla?She is in the bathroom.
Where are they?They are in the computer room.

Things

What is that on the desk?That is a laptop.
Where is my notebook?It is in your bag.
Teacher's Tip

Use is with one person or thing, and are with more than one.

03

Possession

What Is in Your Bag?

Talk about personal objects and ask who owns them.

Singular

Is this your cellphone?Yes, it is.
This is my notebook.Use this for one object near you.

Plural

What are those?They are my earbuds.
Those are your keys.Use those for objects far from you.
